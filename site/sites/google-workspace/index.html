    Google Workspace scrapers and data tools

    Nimbryxo Studio publishes 2 Apify Actors that read Google Workspace. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which Google Workspace Actor do you need?

    • Google Workspace Marketplace App Review Extractor by App ID: Pull public reviews for a specific Google Workspace Marketplace app by its App ID, with reviewer name, star rating, review text, posting date and helpful-vote count, filterable by minimum rating and sort order.
    • Google Workspace Marketplace App Search & Category Browser: Search Google Workspace Marketplace apps by keyword, browse a category, or fetch specific app IDs. Returns developer, rating, review count, pricing model, categories, compatible Google products and icon.

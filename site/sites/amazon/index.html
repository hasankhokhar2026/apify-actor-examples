    Amazon scrapers and data tools

    Nimbryxo Studio publishes 5 Apify Actors that read Amazon. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which Amazon Actor do you need?

    • Amazon All Offers & Buy Box Tracker: Collect every seller offer for Amazon ASINs you supply - condition, price, seller name and rating, shipping terms, delivery estimate, and which offer currently holds the buy box.
    • Amazon Best Sellers & Rankings Scraper: Scrape Amazon's Best Sellers, New Releases, Most Wished For, Most Gifted and Movers & Shakers charts. Get rank, ASIN, title, price, rating and category for every product on a chart, with optional deeper category coverage.
    • Amazon Creator Storefront & Idea List Scraper: Pull Amazon creator storefronts by handle: profile info and affiliate disclosure, every curated Idea List with its products and prices, and shoppable creator videos with tagged products.
    • Amazon Multi-Domain Product Price & Detail Scraper: Pull pricing, ratings, seller, variant and technical detail for Amazon products by URL or ASIN, across 21 country domains, with optional delivery postal-code targeting.
    • Amazon Third-Party Seller Profile & Storefront Catalog: Look up an Amazon third-party seller's public business details, feedback history and ratings histogram, plus every product listed in their storefront, by seller ID or product URL.

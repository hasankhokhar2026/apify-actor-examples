    LinkedIn scrapers and data tools

    Nimbryxo Studio publishes 11 Apify Actors that read LinkedIn. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which LinkedIn Actor do you need?

    • LinkedIn Activity Feed Scraper with Engagement Filters: Pull posts, articles, and reposts from LinkedIn profile activity feeds, with reaction/comment/date filters and a per-reaction-type breakdown. Requires a LinkedIn session cookie.
    • LinkedIn Ad Library Keyword & Advertiser Scraper: Search the public LinkedIn Ad Library by keyword, advertiser, country, or date range, and get headline, body copy, advertiser info, media URLs, and call-to-action for the ads each search returns.
    • LinkedIn Company Headcount & Team Directory Scraper: List current or past employees of any LinkedIn company page with filters for role keyword, seniority, job function, and connection degree. Requires a LinkedIn session cookie.
    • LinkedIn Company Pages with Funding & Affiliates: Pull full LinkedIn company pages, including industry, headcount, headquarters, specialties, and Crunchbase-sourced funding, with optional affiliated-company and showcase-page records for each input company.
    • LinkedIn Job Listings with Salary & Company Data Scraper: Collect LinkedIn job postings by keyword, location, or search URL, with parsed salary ranges, visa-sponsorship signals, and enriched employer profiles. No login required.
    • LinkedIn Jobs Scraper with Salary & Company Enrichment: Search public LinkedIn job listings by keyword, location, salary band, job type and more, with full descriptions, parsed salary ranges, inferred seniority, visa-sponsorship mentions and enriched hiring-company details. No cookies or login required.
    • LinkedIn Learning Course & Learning Path Finder: Search the LinkedIn Learning catalog by keyword for courses, learning paths or videos, with instructor, duration, difficulty, topics and skills taught. No login required.
    • LinkedIn People Finder by Role, Seniority & Network: Search LinkedIn people with structured filters for company, school, seniority, job function, years of experience, industry, and connection degree, using your own LinkedIn session.
    • LinkedIn Post Comment & Reply Thread Extractor: Pull top-level comments from LinkedIn posts, with optional nested reply threads, author identity, reaction counts, and linked profiles found in the comment text.
    • LinkedIn Profile Scraper with Company & Credential Data: Turn a list of public LinkedIn profile URLs into structured records with headline, work history, education, certifications, honors, volunteer work, and current-employer company details.
    • LinkedIn Public Profile Credentials & Company Lookup: Look up public LinkedIn profiles by URL or username and get work history, education, certifications, honors, volunteer work, and projects, with the current employer's own company page details attached. No login or cookies needed.

    Zillow scrapers and data tools

    Nimbryxo Studio publishes 3 Apify Actors that read Zillow. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which Zillow Actor do you need?

    • Zillow For-Sale, Rent & Sold Listing Extractor: Search Zillow by city, state or ZIP for active, rental or recently sold listings, with pricing, Zestimate, agent, school, tax and price history for each property.
    • Zillow Market Trends Scraper: Pull Zillow's home value index, listing and sale price metrics, sale-to-list ratio, days to pending, and rental index for any US city or state, with configurable months of history.
    • Zillow Upcoming Open House Finder by City or ZIP: Find upcoming Zillow open houses by city, ZIP, or search URL, with showing start and end times plus property price, beds/baths, square footage and location.

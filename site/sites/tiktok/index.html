    TikTok scrapers and data tools

    Nimbryxo Studio publishes 8 Apify Actors that read TikTok. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which TikTok Actor do you need?

    • TikTok Explore Feed Trending Post Scraper: Collect trending posts from TikTok's Explore feed across one or more content categories, with engagement stats, author snapshot, sound and video details. Can also try to discover category IDs.
    • TikTok Follow Graph & Audience Export Scraper: Export who a TikTok account follows, or who follows it, as full public profile records with bio, verification status and exact engagement stats. No login or cookie is needed for public accounts.
    • TikTok Hashtag Video Feed & Stats Extractor: Collect a TikTok hashtag's total view/video counts plus the video feed underneath it - captions, author, music, and engagement - with automatic fallback when the feed is blocked.
    • TikTok LIVE Room Status & Chat Capture Tool: Check TikTok usernames for an active LIVE stream and get room title, viewer counts, and host profile, or capture real-time chat, gifts, likes, and other events while the stream runs.
    • TikTok Post & Slideshow Detail Scraper: Fetch full metadata for any TikTok video or photo-carousel post by URL or ID: engagement stats, author snapshot, music details, hashtags, mentions, and signed media URLs.
    • TikTok Top Ads Leaderboard - 122 Markets & Metrics: Collect TikTok Creative Center Top Ads across 122 markets with 258 industries, 7 rankings, landing pages, retention curves, and Spotlight. No login or cookies; sweeps past TikTok's 20-ad board up to 500 unique ads.
    • TikTok Verified Mention Tracker: Find TikTok posts that tag a specific creator or brand handle, with each result checked against TikTok's own mention markup so you can tell a real @mention from a caption that merely contains the name.
    • TikTok Video, Audio & Cover Downloader: Download TikTok videos, cover thumbnails, background audio, and slideshow images by post URL, with quality selection and a stable download link per file.

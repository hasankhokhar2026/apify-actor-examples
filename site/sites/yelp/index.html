    Yelp scrapers and data tools

    Nimbryxo Studio publishes 2 Apify Actors that read Yelp. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which Yelp Actor do you need?

    • Yelp Business Profile, Hours & Menu Extractor: Search Yelp or fetch specific business URLs for profile data, hours, services, photo galleries, owner replies, coordinates, and reviews with reaction counts.
    • Yelp Business Search & Review Scraper: Search Yelp by keyword and location, or scrape specific business pages directly, to collect business details, ratings, categories, contact details, and customer reviews.

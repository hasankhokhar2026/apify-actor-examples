    YouTube scrapers and data tools

    Nimbryxo Studio publishes 7 Apify Actors that read YouTube. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which YouTube Actor do you need?

    • YouTube Ad Creative & Advertiser Tracker: Find the video and image ad creatives an advertiser is running on YouTube, discovered by keyword, advertiser name, or advertiser ID, with country, format, and first/last-shown dates.
    • YouTube Caption & Whisper AI Transcript Extractor: Get timestamped transcripts from YouTube videos in your preferred language, with optional AI transcription for videos that have no captions at all. Returns full text, timed segments, and core video metadata.
    • YouTube Channel Contact Email Finder: Find publicly listed contact emails for YouTube channels by scanning each About page and the Instagram, TikTok, and Linktree profiles it links to. No login or API key needed.
    • YouTube Channel Video Feed Quick Scraper: Quickly list any YouTube channel's videos, sorted newest, most popular or oldest, with channel subscriber count, verification and optional Shorts, playlists and live-stream tabs.
    • YouTube Trending Feed Extractor by Category & Market: Get video lists for YouTube's Gaming, Music, News, Movies and Trending sections in any of 31 regional markets, with views, duration and channel details; rows from a fallback search are labeled.
    • YouTube Video Comment & Reply Thread Scraper: Get a YouTube video's comments with nested replies attached, author identity, like counts, and pinned/hearted/verified flags, filterable by keyword and minimum likes.
    • YouTube Video, Playlist & Channel Downloader with Metadata: Download YouTube videos from direct links, playlists, channels, or search phrases at your chosen quality, with a public file link plus title, stats, description and subtitles per video.

    Facebook scrapers and data tools

    Nimbryxo Studio publishes 5 Apify Actors that read Facebook. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which Facebook Actor do you need?

    • Facebook Ad Library Byline & Advertiser Ad Finder: Pull Facebook Ad Library ads by advertiser Page ID, keyword, Ad ID, or "Paid for by" byline, with ad text, media, run dates, creative-reuse counts, and spend or impression ranges for political ads.
    • Facebook Business Page Finder by Keyword & City: Find Facebook business pages by direct URL, or discover them by keyword and city, and get names, categories, addresses, follower counts, contact details, business hours, ratings, and reviews. A Facebook login cookie is recommended for full coverage.
    • Facebook Business Page Recommendation & Review Scraper: Get Facebook business page reviews by direct URL, business name search, or Page ID, with Recommended/Not Recommended status, owner replies, and filters for date, keyword, and recommendation status.
    • Facebook Public Follower & Following Snapshot Tool: Get the public preview of a Facebook Page or Profile's followers, following, or friends, plus aggregate follower/following counts and parsed location, workplace, or school details.
    • Facebook Timeline Posts & Reel Transcript Extractor: Pull recent posts and Reels from a public Facebook Page or Profile, or fetch one post by its link, with text, reaction breakdown, comments, media, and real auto-generated video captions. No login or cookies required.

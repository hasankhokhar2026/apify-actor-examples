    ImportYeti Importer & Supplier Trade Explorer: export to CSV, Excel or JSON

    Search ImportYeti by company, supplier, product, address, HS code, location or product tag; optionally open profiles for top trading partners, recent bills of lading, HS/HTS codes, shipping lanes, carriers, containers, notify parties and UFLPA flags.

    This tutorial runs ImportYeti Importer & Supplier Trade Explorer through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON.

    What people export it for

    • Find the overseas companies shipping a product category to the US.
    • Map a competitor's top suppliers and recent import activity.
    • Build a list of US importers for an HS code or a location.
    • Check a partner's shipment recency, volume and trade lanes before outreach.
    • Research HS/HTS codes, carriers and port pairs behind a company's imports.

    Fix common problems

    Why did I get fewer rows than maxResults?

    The source may have fewer matches, or the query hit the 280-row search ceiling. Add another query, use an HS/location/tag mode, or lower the filters.

    Why are phone numbers and emails missing?

    ImportYeti redacts them for visitors who are not logged in. The actor omits masked contact fields rather than guessing.

    Why are there only 50 shipments for a large importer?

    ImportYeti publishes the 50 most recent bills of lading per company or supplier. Older shipment detail is not on the public page.

    Facebook Business Page Finder by Keyword & City: export to CSV, Excel or JSON

    Find Facebook business pages by direct URL, or discover them by keyword and city, and get names, categories, addresses, follower counts, contact details, business hours, ratings, and reviews. A Facebook login cookie is recommended for full coverage.

    This tutorial runs Facebook Business Page Finder by Keyword & City through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON, keeping the 8 columns its publisher puts first: pageName, category, address, followers, recommendPercentage, facebookUrl, pageUrl and pageId.

    Fix common problems

    • HTTP 401: the token is missing or wrong; run echo $APIFY_TOKEN in the same shell.
    • An empty file: open the run in Console and read its log; it usually names the input that matched nothing.
    • Odd characters in Excel: open the CSV through Data, From Text/CSV and pick UTF-8, or download xlsx instead.

    Google Workspace Marketplace App Search & Category Browser export

    Search Google Workspace Marketplace apps by keyword, browse a category, or fetch specific app IDs. Returns developer, rating, review count, pricing model, categories, compatible Google products and icon.

    This tutorial runs Google Workspace Marketplace App Search & Category Browser through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON, keeping the 8 columns its publisher puts first: appId, name, developer, rating, reviewCount, installCount, pricingModel and worksWith.

    What people export it for

    • Market mapping - enumerate the whole Marketplace to size a category or find every app in a workflow niche
    • Competitive research - compare installs, ratings, pricing and feature positioning across rivals
    • Vendor and lead discovery - collect developer names, websites and support contacts from app listings
    • Review monitoring - export recent low-star reviews for product-quality or support analysis
    • Ecosystem tracking - follow a publisher's app portfolio and detect new listings

    Fix common problems

    Why is a search limited to 100 apps?

    The Marketplace serves a fixed top-100 list per query and ignores pagination parameters. Use a narrower query, or use browseAll to enumerate the whole catalogue.

    Why did an app's price disappear?

    The listing publishes a price class only for some apps. When it says "Not available", the field is omitted instead of guessing.

    Why are categories not in the output?

    The Marketplace does not expose per-app category names on an app's page or in its data. Category membership is only visible by browsing category pages, so the actor does not invent it.

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.

    Google Maps Review & Contributor Activity Scraper: CSV and Excel export

    Get reviews for any Google Maps business or place, or switch to author mode to pull everything one Google reviewer has written. Includes ratings, review text, dates, likes, and owner replies.

    This tutorial runs Google Maps Review & Contributor Activity Scraper through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON, keeping the 8 columns its publisher puts first: recordType, reviewerName, rating, reviewText, reviewDate, likesCount, businessName and sortBy.

    What people export it for

    • Reputation tracking for branches and franchises: schedule a monitoring run over every location and get only the new reviews each morning.
    • Competitor and market research: run a search for a category and city, then compare rating breakdowns, topics and complaints.
    • Customer-experience analytics: feed full texts, translations and sub-ratings into sentiment or topic models.
    • Lead generation for agencies: find businesses with recent low ratings or without owner replies.
    • Trust and safety: review a reviewer's whole activity, spot unusual patterns, and see which places they reviewed and when.

    Fix common problems

    Do I need to set up a proxy?

    No. The actor chooses its connection itself and switches automatically if Google slows it down. There are no proxy settings to configure.

    Why does a place give fewer reviews than its total?

    Google's total also counts reviews it no longer displays. On a full newest-first collection the actor reads everything Google still lists, and the place summary shows exactly how many reviews were read and why collection stopped.

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.

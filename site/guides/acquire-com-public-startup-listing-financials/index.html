    What people export it for

    • Deal sourcing — filter listings by category, price and multiple to build a shortlist of acquisition targets
    • Due diligence prep — pull a specific listing's full financials and highlights before reaching out to the seller
    • Market benchmarking — compare revenue and profit multiples across categories to gauge fair pricing
    • Growth-stage screening — filter on minAnnualGrowthRate to find fast-growing SaaS or ecommerce businesses for sale
    • Advisory/verification screening — isolate listings under Acquire advisory support or flagged as verified for lower-risk sourcing

    Fix common problems

    Why are fields like churn rate, team structure, or raw analytics screenshots never returned?

    Those live behind Acquire's authenticated in-app view and aren't part of the public page; the actor only emits fields it can read from the public listing.

    Why does sortBy rely on sitemap freshness rather than a "newest" field on the listing?

    The sitemap's lastmod is the only cross-category freshness signal available without logging in; price-based sorts over-fetch listings and re-sort them after scraping.

    Why is revenueMultiple sometimes missing even though askingPrice is present?

    It's only computed when the listing also reports annual revenue; without both numbers there's nothing to divide.

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.

    YouTube Caption & Whisper AI Transcript Extractor: CSV and Excel export

    Get timestamped transcripts from YouTube videos in your preferred language, with optional AI transcription for videos that have no captions at all. Returns full text, timed segments, and core video metadata.

    This tutorial runs YouTube Caption & Whisper AI Transcript Extractor through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON, keeping the 8 columns its publisher puts first: video_id, title, channel_name, publishedAt, transcript_language, segment_count, success and transcript_available.

    What people export it for

    • AI and RAG pipelines — feed clean, timestamped transcripts into language models, search indexes, and knowledge bases.
    • Content repurposing — turn webinars, interviews, and long-form videos into articles, summaries, and social posts.
    • Subtitling and localization — export SRT/VTT and translate transcripts for multilingual audiences.
    • Media monitoring — follow a channel or playlist and collect everything its creators publish.
    • Research and journalism — quote speakers accurately with timestamps, chapters, and exact publish dates.
    • Education and accessibility — build searchable transcripts for lectures, courses, and public talks.

    Fix common problems

    Do I need to set up a proxy?

    No. The actor chooses a working connection to YouTube automatically. Just fill in the input and run it.

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.

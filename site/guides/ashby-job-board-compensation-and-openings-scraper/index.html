    Ashby Job Board Compensation & Openings Scraper: export to CSV, Excel or JSON

    Scrape public Ashby job boards for any company hiring on jobs.ashbyhq.com. Get every open role with title, department, team, location, remote status, salary/compensation ranges, apply links, and full job descriptions. No login, no API key.

    What people export it for

    • Job boards and aggregators — keep a live feed of openings from every Ashby-powered company you track.
    • Recruiting and sourcing — find engineering, design, sales, or operations roles by team, location, and workplace type.
    • Compensation benchmarking — collect the employer's own salary ranges as numbers, with currency and pay period.
    • Remote-work feeds — build a daily feed of fully remote roles with remote-eligible countries.
    • Hiring intelligence — compare departments, teams, locations, and pay coverage across competitors.
    • Market research — track which companies are hiring and where over time.

    Fix common problems

    Do I need an API key, login, or proxy?

    No. The Ashby job-board endpoint is public. No credentials, cookies, or proxy setup are needed.

    Why is isRemote true for a hybrid job?

    Ashby's remote flag means "remote-friendly", not "fully remote". Use workplaceType: "Remote" for fully remote roles and workplaceType: "Hybrid" for hybrid roles.

    Why do some jobs have no salary?

    Companies choose whether to publish compensation. When they do not, the salary fields are omitted. This actor never guesses or fabricates a range.

    More from the publisher:

    • Why are unlisted jobs missing?
    • Why does the run finish with fewer records than the board has?

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.

    Product Hunt Launch & Topic Data Exporter: export to CSV, Excel or JSON

    Export Product Hunt launches with votes, comments, makers, hunter and topics from the daily leaderboard, a topic, a maker's history, or specific product slugs.

    This tutorial runs Product Hunt Launch & Topic Data Exporter through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON, keeping the 8 columns its publisher puts first: name, tagline, votesCount, commentsCount, reviewsCount, reviewsRating, dailyRank and featuredAt.

    Fix common problems

    Why do I get more than 20 launches per day?

    The visible leaderboard is a preview. The actor follows the cursor until the source is exhausted, which is normally 700-950 launches on a busy day.

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.

    What people export it for

    • Salary benchmarking: compare yearly pay for a title across cities, countries and employers.
    • Recruiting and sourcing: watch fresh postings in your niche, see who is hiring and who posted each role.
    • Sales prospecting: spot companies that are hiring fast in a segment, with their size, website and offices.
    • Job hunting: a daily run with "Past 24 hours", Easy Apply and fewer than 10 applicants, skipping jobs you've already seen.
    • Labour-market research: track demand, seniority mix and pay transparency over time.

    Fix common problems

    Why do some jobs have no salary?

    Many employers don't publish pay. The actor reads LinkedIn's pay box and pay ranges written in the description, and never estimates. salarySource shows where each figure came from. Expect most US jobs in pay-transparency states to have a salary, and far fewer elsewhere.

    Why can't I get the employer's own application link?

    LinkedIn only shows the external "Apply on company website" link to signed-in members. applyUrl is the LinkedIn page where the application starts, and easyApply tells you whether you can apply on LinkedIn directly.

    My location returned the wrong country. LinkedIn quietly searches the whole United States when it doesn't recognise a place. The actor checks every location first: an unrecognised one is matched to LinkedIn's closest suggestion or skipped with a note in the status message. Type places in English ("Germany", not "Deutschland"), or use geoIds.

    Do I need to set up a proxy?

    No. The actor picks its connection automatically and switches when LinkedIn slows it down.

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.

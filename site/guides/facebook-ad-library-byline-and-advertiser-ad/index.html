    Facebook Ad Library Byline & Advertiser Ad Finder: CSV and Excel export

    Pull Facebook Ad Library ads by advertiser Page ID, keyword, Ad ID, or "Paid for by" byline, with ad text, media, run dates, creative-reuse counts, and spend or impression ranges for political ads.

    This tutorial runs Facebook Ad Library Byline & Advertiser Ad Finder through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON, keeping the 8 columns its publisher puts first: ad_id, page_name, ad_text, start_date, status, media_type, cta_text and link_url.

    What people export it for

    • Competitive ad monitoring: pull the current rotation for a competitor Page and compare hooks, offers and landing pages.
    • Creative research: collect live ad copy, images, video and carousel cards for a swipe file or mood board.
    • Political and issue-ad accountability: follow a "Paid for by" committee, then read its spend, impressions and payer/beneficiary data.
    • EU DSA transparency: audit an advertiser's EU reach, targeting and demographic breakdown.
    • Creative-reuse analysis: sort by collation_count to see which creative a brand is scaling hardest.
    • Known-ad due diligence: resolve a specific Ad Library ID to its full record before it disappears from the live library.

    Fix common problems

    Why are spend and impressions missing on some ads?

    Meta only publishes those figures for political/issue ads and some EU/UK transparency ads. The actor never estimates or fills them for ordinary commercial ads; the fields are omitted instead.

    Why is a media URL missing or different?

    Facebook's own media URLs are signed and expire after a few days. With rehostMedia on, the actor copies the primary media (up to 15 MB) to the run's key-value store and returns a permanent URL in media_url, keeping the original in media_url_original and its expiry in media_url_original_expires_at. media_rehost_status tells you whether the copy succeeded; if it is too_large, download_failed or kv_failed, media_url remains the signed original. Per-card, array and Page profile/cover URLs stay as Meta served them.

    Why did a byline search return nothing?

    Meta's byline filter matches the funding-entity string exactly and only works inside the political archive. The actor tries the uppercase form first, then the original casing, and also re-checks each returned ad's byline field client-side. If nothing matches, you get a diagnostic rather than an unrelated ad.

    More from the publisher:

    • Why did an EU political or byline search get remapped?
    • Does the actor need a proxy?

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.

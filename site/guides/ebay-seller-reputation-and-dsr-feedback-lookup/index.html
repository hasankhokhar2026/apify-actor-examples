    eBay Seller Reputation & DSR Feedback Lookup: export to CSV, Excel or JSON

    Look up any eBay seller by username or store URL and get individual feedback entries (rating, comment, item, price, buyer) plus their overall reputation stats and Detailed Seller Ratings.

    This tutorial runs eBay Seller Reputation & DSR Feedback Lookup through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON, keeping the 8 columns its publisher puts first: sellerUsername, rating, comment, dateRange, itemTitle, priceFormatted, buyerUsername and verifiedPurchase.

    What people export it for

    • Seller due diligence — read recent feedback, negative comments, replies and DSR scores before buying
    • Reputation audits — track a seller's positive percentage, rating counts and DSR averages over time
    • Competitive benchmarking — compare feedback volume, positive share and DSR across sellers
    • Complaint mining — pull negative or neutral feedback and surface recurring shipping, condition or communication issues
    • Marketplace analytics — collect the same seller across multiple eBay marketplaces
    • Research datasets — export feedback rows with item, price and seller context for analysis

    Fix common problems

    Q: Why is the buyer name partially hidden?

    A: eBay masks buyer names on public feedback profiles for privacy. The actor reports exactly what eBay shows.

    Q: Why is there no exact feedback date?

    A: eBay publishes only relative time buckets for individual feedback entries on the public profile.

    Q: Is there a row limit?

    A: Yes. maxItems caps rows across the run, maxFeedbackPerSeller caps one seller and maxPages is a safety cap on pagination. eBay may also stop serving older pages.

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.

    Bar Standards Board Barrister Register Scraper: export to CSV, Excel or JSON

    Search the Bar Standards Board's official Barristers' Register for England & Wales by name or chambers, filter by practising status, area of practice, or public access, and browse disciplinary findings and suspensions.

    This tutorial runs Bar Standards Board Barrister Register Scraper through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON, keeping the 8 columns its publisher puts first: recordType, barristerName, currentPractisingStatus, memberStatus, dateOfCall, innOfCourt, primaryPracticeAddress and areasOfPractice.

    What people export it for

    • Due diligence — verify a barrister's practising status, certificate validity, Inn of Court and disciplinary history before instructing them
    • Legal directories and comparison sites — import the full authorised-practising register with chambers, contact details and practice areas
    • Compliance monitoring — track new disciplinary findings, sanctions and interim suspensions as they are published
    • Recruitment and chambers research — size practice areas, identify Public Access or litigation-authorised barristers, and map chambers
    • Regulatory analytics — measure the distribution of practising statuses, areas of practice and reserved activities across England & Wales
    • Journalism and legal research — cross-reference a finding's sanction, costs and published decision documents with case reporting

    Fix common problems

    Why do some barrister records have fewer fields than others?

    The register only publishes certain details for certain statuses. For example, a disbarred barrister has no current chambers address, so that field is omitted rather than shown as empty.

    Do I need to set up a proxy?

    No. The actor connects to the public register directly. Just fill in the input and run it.

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.

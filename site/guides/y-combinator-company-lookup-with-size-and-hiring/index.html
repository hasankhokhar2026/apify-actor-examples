    Y Combinator Company Lookup with Size & Hiring Filters: CSV and Excel export

    Look up Y Combinator companies by direct URL or slug, or filter the directory by team size, founding year, hiring status and founder social presence, with each company's founders, open jobs and social links attached.

    This tutorial runs Y Combinator Company Lookup with Size & Hiring Filters through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON, keeping the 8 columns its publisher puts first: name, slug, batch, industry, ycdcStatus, teamSize, companyAge and hasOpenJobs.

    What people export it for

    • VC and angel sourcing — shortlist companies in a size and funding-stage window within a batch
    • Recruiting pipelines — pull hiring companies and their open roles in a target industry
    • Direct company research — fetch a known set of companies by slug without running a search
    • Founder outreach — filter to companies whose founders already have a public LinkedIn or Twitter
    • Diversity-focused sourcing — isolate companies YC has flagged under its founder-diversity highlights

    Fix common problems

    Why would jobCount differ from what I see on YC's site?

    It reflects the exact set of open postings YC's own company profile returns at the time of the run, which can shift as listings open and close.

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.

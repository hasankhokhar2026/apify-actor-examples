    Zomato City Restaurant & Cuisine Filter Scraper is an Apify Actor published by Nimbryxo Studio. It accepts 15 input fields, none of them required at the top level. Each result can carry up to 8 declared fields, including name, cuisines, ratings.aggregate, price.for_two, location.locality and location.city. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Zomato lists tens of thousands of restaurants across India and the UAE, but its public pages cap every listing view at nine results and offer no export option. This actor turns a Zomato city, cuisine, or locality page — or a filter combination you choose — into structured restaurant records with ratings, cost, location, and contact details already parsed out.

    What this actor does

    • Accepts either direct Zomato URLs (city, cuisine, locality, delivery/cafe/nightlife pages) or a city + filter combination that it turns into a URL for you.
    • Applies cuisine, category, minimum rating, sort order, and cost-for-two filters, matching Zomato's own on-site filter options.
    • Optionally fetches each restaurant's own detail page to add phone numbers, opening hours, top dishes, known-for tags, and feature flags on top of the search-result basics.
    • Can fan a single city out across its top cuisines or its locality pages so one run covers far more ground than one URL would return alone.
    • Returns every field for every restaurant; a field with nothing to report comes back as an empty string, 0, or an empty list rather than being left out of the record.

    Use cases

    • Food-delivery market mapping — compare cuisine coverage, pricing, and ratings across neighbourhoods before entering a city.
    • Lead generation for restaurant tech vendors — build contact lists of restaurants by city and cuisine, complete with phone numbers.
    • Competitive pricing research — pull cost-for-two and rating data for a cuisine or category to benchmark a new menu or concept.
    • Travel and dining guides — source restaurant details, hours, and highlights for a locality to build curated city guides.
    • Academic and market research — track how ratings, pricing, and feature availability (outdoor seating, home delivery) vary by city.

    Questions

    How many restaurants can one run return?

    Each Zomato page returns at most 9 restaurants — that's a limit on Zomato's own listing pages, not something this actor can extend per page. Use multiple start_urls, or turn on expand_cuisines / expand_localities to combine many pages into one run.

    Which cities are covered?

    Mumbai, NCR (Delhi), Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, and Lucknow in India, plus Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and Sharjah in the UAE — the markets where Zomato still operates. Both the city filter builder and start_urls are limited to these cities; a URL for a city outside this list is skipped.

    What's the difference between start_urls and city?

    start_urls lets you paste exact Zomato pages, including ones with cuisine or locality already baked into the path. city instead builds a search URL for you from the search_mode, cuisine, category, min_rating, sort, and cost fields. You can use either, or both, in the same run.

    Why would ratings.delivery or price.for_two be 0?

    Those values are 0 when Zomato itself doesn't publish them for that restaurant — for example, a dine-in-only place has no delivery rating, and some listings omit a published cost for two.

    What do the expand_cuisines and expand_localities toggles actually fetch?

    expand_cuisines queues one page per top cuisine for the chosen city; expand_localities queues one page per locality Zomato lists for that city. Both multiply the number of pages fetched, and therefore the number of restaurants returned, for a single city.

    Does turning off enrich_details lose data?

    Yes — with enrich_details off, the output is limited to what's visible on the listing page (name, cuisines, locality, rating, cost). Phone numbers, opening hours, top dishes, known-for tags, and feature flags all come from the restaurant's own detail page.

    Are menu items or review text included?

    No. Zomato's current public pages don't expose a structured menu or individual review text, so those aren't part of the output.

    Is this affiliated with Zomato?

    No. This actor reads Zomato's public pages and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Zomato in any way.

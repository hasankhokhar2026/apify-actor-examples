Yelp Business Profiles & Review History Extractor

Yelp packs a lot into each business page: star ratings, price signals, weekly hours, photo galleries, contact details, and years of customer reviews. This actor turns that material into structured business records, with the review feed paged well beyond the handful of reviews Yelp shows by default.

What this actor does

  • Finds businesses by keyword and location, or by a Yelp search URL that already contains filters.
  • Looks up exact businesses from a Yelp business URL, a bare alias such as prince-street-pizza-new-york-2, or a 22-character Yelp business ID.
  • Pages each business's review feed up to your chosen limit, including reviewer names, Elite years, photos, reactions, and owner replies.
  • Adds search metadata so you can tell organic ranking from sponsored placement and remove duplicate cards.
  • Omits empty fields at every level. A business that does not publish a website, price level, or owner reply simply has no such key in its record.
  • Writes an explicit status record when a business is missing, a page is blocked, or Yelp changes the data contract, so an empty dataset is never mistaken for a successful scrape.

Status records

When something prevents a business or search page from being collected, the dataset receives a row with recordType: "status":

  • status is not_found, blocked, api_drift, no_results, or invalid_input.
  • message explains what happened.
  • sourceUrl and businessId are included when known.
  • scrapedAt records when the status was written.

Legacy compatibility keys are also present when they have values: directUrl (same as url), bizId (same as alias), aggregatedRating (rating as text), priceRange (same as price), and images (same as photos). Review rows also keep isUsefulCount, isFunnyCount, and isCoolCount beside the reactions object.

Status rows are separate from business rows, so filter on recordType before loading the business records.

Use cases

  • Competitive benchmarking — compare ratings, review volume, price levels, and review themes across a set of local businesses.
  • Review monitoring — schedule runs against your own locations and watch new reviews, owner replies, and rating changes over time.
  • Lead research — collect phone numbers, websites, cities, and categories for businesses in a target market.
  • Voice-of-customer analysis — export hundreds of reviews per business with ratings, dates, languages, and reviewer context.
  • Location planning — map business density and quality across ZIP codes, neighborhoods, or categories.
  • Data enrichment — join Yelp business IDs, aliases, websites, and coordinates to an existing CRM or dataset.

FAQ

Do I need to supply a proxy? No. The actor starts with a direct request and automatically routes through Apify Proxy when Yelp blocks it. Leave proxyConfiguration at its default, or supply a country or custom proxy only when you need a specific route.

How many businesses can one search return? Yelp caps a single search at 240 businesses, displayed 10 at a time. maxItems can be as high as 500 across multiple queries or direct URLs, but each individual search stops at Yelp's 240-result ceiling.

How many reviews can I collect per business? Up to 1,000. The review feed is paged at 49 reviews per request, so a deep run uses several requests per business. Set maxReviewsPerBusiness lower for faster, cheaper runs.

Why is reviewsTotal sometimes lower than reviewCount? Yelp's profile count and its public review feed are not identical. The feed leaves out reviews Yelp does not recommend, so reviewsTotal can be slightly lower than the number shown on the page.

What does reviewLanguage: "all" do? The actor reads the language counts Yelp reports for the business and pages each language until the review limit is reached, de-duplicating by review ID. It can use several requests per business.

Why is a business or review field missing? Yelp does not publish every field for every business. Missing values are omitted rather than written as null, empty strings, or empty arrays.

What happens when Yelp changes its data format? The actor writes an api_drift status row with a message and the source URL, and the run status message reports the problem. It does not silently return an empty dataset.

Can I use a Yelp search URL with filters already in it? Yes. Put it in startUrls with mode=search; the actor keeps the URL's query parameters and pages from its existing start position.

Does this actor collect the reviews Yelp does not recommend? No. It collects the public review feed. Reviews outside that feed are not exposed, which is why the feed total can differ from the profile count.

Is this affiliated with Yelp? No. This is an independent third-party actor and is not endorsed by or affiliated with Yelp.

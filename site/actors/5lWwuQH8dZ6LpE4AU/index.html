Glassdoor Employee Reviews with Full Ratings Breakdown

Pull employee reviews from any company's Glassdoor reviews page, including the overall rating and every sub-rating category, pros, cons, advice to management, and reviewer details such as job title and employment status.

What this actor does

  • Reads a Glassdoor company reviews page by URL and extracts each review shown on it
  • Captures the overall rating plus six sub-ratings (work-life balance, culture and values, diversity and inclusion, senior leadership, career opportunities, compensation and benefits)
  • Returns reviewer context: job title, location, employment status, and whether they're a current or former employee
  • Sorts results by newest first or by relevance when a session cookie is supplied
  • Accepts an optional session cookie to read beyond the handful of reviews Glassdoor shows to logged-out visitors

Output per review

Field Description
reviewId Glassdoor's unique review ID
reviewDate Timestamp when the review was posted
title Review headline
pros What the reviewer liked
cons What the reviewer disliked
advice Advice to management, when the reviewer left any
ratingOverall Overall star rating (1-5)
ratingWorkLifeBalance Work-life balance rating
ratingCultureAndValues Culture and values rating
ratingDiversityAndInclusion Diversity and inclusion rating
ratingSeniorLeadership Senior leadership rating
ratingCareerOpportunities Career opportunities rating
ratingCompensationAndBenefits Compensation and benefits rating
ratingCeo CEO approval, e.g. "APPROVE", "DISAPPROVE", "NO_OPINION"
ratingBusinessOutlook Business outlook sentiment, e.g. "POSITIVE", "NEGATIVE", "NEUTRAL"
ratingRecommendToFriend Whether the reviewer would recommend the company to a friend
jobTitle Reviewer's job title
location Reviewer's work location
employmentStatus Employment type, e.g. "REGULAR", "PART_TIME", "INTERN"
isCurrentJob true if the reviewer currently works there
lengthOfEmployment Length of employment, in months
countHelpful Number of "helpful" votes the review received
countNotHelpful Number of "not helpful" votes
companyName Company name
companyId Glassdoor's internal company ID

Every review record has all of the fields above. When Glassdoor doesn't show a value, the rating and count fields (ratingWorkLifeBalance … ratingCompensationAndBenefits, lengthOfEmployment, countHelpful, countNotHelpful) are 0 and the text fields (advice, ratingCeo, ratingBusinessOutlook, ratingRecommendToFriend, jobTitle, location, employmentStatus) are an empty string, so treat 0 sub-ratings as "not given". This happens most often on reviews read without a session cookie.

Error records: if companyUrl is missing or isn't a Glassdoor /Reviews/ URL, or no review could be extracted in the run, the dataset gets a single record instead: {type: "glassdoor_scraper_error", reason, message, companyUrl, scrapedAt}, where reason is invalid_input or anti_bot_blocked (and companyUrl is left out when none was given).

Input

Field Type Default Description
companyUrl string (required) – Glassdoor company reviews page URL, e.g. https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/Google-Reviews-E9079.htm
maxItems integer 100 Maximum number of reviews to scrape (1-1000). Each run stops after about four and a half minutes, so very high values are usually not reached in one run
sort string DATE Review order: DATE (newest first) or RELEVANCE (most relevant first). Only applied when a cookie is supplied; without one, reviews come in the page's default order
cookie string – Optional Glassdoor session cookies, as a name=value; name2=value2 string or a JSON cookie export, used to page through more reviews

Example: quick preview, no cookie

{
  "companyUrl": "https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/Google-Reviews-E9079.htm",
  "maxItems": 10,
  "sort": "DATE"
}

Example: full pagination with a session cookie

{
  "companyUrl": "https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/Google-Reviews-E9079.htm",
  "maxItems": 200,
  "sort": "DATE",
  "cookie": "GSESSIONID=abc123; gdId=xyz789"
}

Use cases

  • Employer reputation research — read what employees actually say about pay, management and culture
  • Competitive benchmarking — compare sub-ratings like compensation or work-life balance across similar employers
  • Due diligence — check recent sentiment before an investment, partnership or acquisition
  • Hiring and retention insight — surface recurring pros and cons mentioned by current and former staff
  • Sentiment tracking over time — re-run periodically (with a cookie and date sort) to watch how new reviews trend

FAQ

Do I need a Glassdoor account? No, not for a basic run — the actor reads the small set of preview reviews Glassdoor shows to visitors who aren't logged in (usually only a few per company). A session cookie is only needed to read further into a company's review history.

How do I get a session cookie? Log into Glassdoor in your browser, open its developer tools, find the cookies for glassdoor.com, and copy the cookie string into the cookie input. The field is marked secret and is never included in the output.

Why are some reviews missing sub-ratings? The handful of preview reviews available without a cookie often carry less detail than the full history. Supplying a session cookie typically returns reviews with complete sub-ratings.

What does isCurrentJob tell me? It reflects whether the reviewer stated they still work at the company at the time of writing the review, as shown on Glassdoor.

What if a run returns no reviews? You'll get one glassdoor_scraper_error record instead. invalid_input means the URL isn't a Glassdoor Reviews page; anti_bot_blocked means Glassdoor didn't serve reviews during that run. Re-running, or supplying a session cookie, often resolves the second case.

Why did I get fewer reviews than maxItems? Without a cookie only the preview reviews are available. With a cookie, the run still stops after about four and a half minutes, or after three pages in a row come back empty.

Can I scrape multiple companies in one run? Each run takes a single companyUrl; run the actor once per company to build a multi-company dataset.

Is this affiliated with Glassdoor? No. This actor reads Glassdoor's public pages and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Glassdoor.

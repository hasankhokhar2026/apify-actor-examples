    Amazon Creator Storefront & Idea List Scraper is an Apify Actor published by Nimbryxo Studio. It accepts 13 input fields, none of them required at the top level. Each result can carry up to 11 declared fields, including recordType, handle, status, displayName, title and asin. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Amazon-approved creators run public affiliate storefronts at amazon.com/shop/{handle}. This actor turns a creator handle (or a full storefront URL) into structured records: the creator profile and social links, every Idea List with its products, shoppable photos and videos, curations, and the storefront's trending picks. No login is required — these are public pages.

    What this actor does

    • Creator profile — display name, bio, profile/cover image, affiliate disclosure text, Top Creator badge, total post count, and linked social profiles (TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Instagram, and others when shown)
    • Idea Lists — every curated collection with its title, cover image, declared item count, pinned flag, heart count, and last-updated text
    • Idea List products — ASIN, title, brand, price, list price, delivery text, badge, availability evidence, and creator comments where shown
    • Shoppable photos — photo URL, image, heart count, and pinned flag
    • Creator videos — title, duration, HLS stream, thumbnail, tagged/related ASINs, product count, heart count, captions, and canonical video page URL
    • Curations — themed post collections with title, post count, cover image, and URL
    • Trending picks — the product carousel with ASIN, image, price, and badge
    • Post search — optional case-insensitive title filter over the creator's Idea Lists and videos (photos have no title, so they are omitted while a search is active)
    • Multi-marketplace — 23 Amazon domains, with each shopUrls entry reporting its own marketplace envelope
    • Honest omissions — the dataset never contains null, "", or []; fields the source does not show are omitted

    Data source

    All data comes from Amazon's public creator storefront pages and their public same-origin AJAX endpoints. The actor does not log in, does not use cookies, and does not access private creator dashboards or Associates account data.

    Use cases

    • Affiliate/influencer research — see which products a creator recommends and at what price point
    • Competitive monitoring — track a brand's creator partnerships and changing product picks
    • Product discovery — mine curated Idea Lists, trending picks, and shoppable media for niche products
    • Content strategy — compare creators by catalog size, video engagement, and curation themes

    This actor emits seven record types (recordType field) in one dataset. Empty fields are omitted at every depth; fields the source does not publish are never emitted as null, blank or placeholders.

    Shop profile (recordType: "shopProfile")

    • handle, shopUrl
    • status — ACTIVE, INACTIVE_STOREFRONT, NOT_FOUND, or BLOCKED
    • displayName, bio, profileImage, coverImage
    • affiliateDisclosure — verbatim disclosure text the creator shows
    • isTopCreator — true when Amazon renders the Top Creator badge
    • totalPosts — the storefront-wide post total shown in the search box label
    • socialLinks[] — {platform, url} for each linked social profile

    Questions

    What is a creator/influencer storefront?

    It is a public Amazon page (amazon.com/shop/{handle}) where an approved Amazon Influencer curates products into an affiliate-linked profile. It is distinct from a Brand Store (run by a manufacturer) and a third-party seller's storefront.

    Why do declaredItemCount and extractedItemCount sometimes differ?

    Amazon's Idea Lists can include items that are unavailable in a variant/style and show as "may be unavailable" placeholders, and the product grid can lazily render. We report both numbers honestly rather than forcing them to match.

    What happens for an inactive creator shop?

    Amazon shows the explicit text "This Influencer Storefront is not active." The actor emits one shopProfile record with status: "INACTIVE_STOREFRONT" and stops for that handle.

    Do I need a proxy or login?

    No login is required. The actor starts with a direct connection, falls back to the free AUTO proxy group, and escalates to residential only after repeated real blocks. You never need to configure a proxy.

    Does this work on non-US Amazon marketplaces?

    Yes — marketplaceDomain and shopUrls accept all 23 Amazon domains. A creator may have a different catalog on each marketplace; each shopUrls entry reports its own real marketplace envelope. Some non-US storefronts sit behind an AWS WAF challenge that the actor retries past.

    Why do product, list, or curation URLs return a 503 when I curl them directly?

    Amazon's product (/dp/{asin}) and storefront list/curation pages reject some non-browser or cookieless requests with a 503. The links are valid and open normally in a browser; it is an Amazon-side anti-bot check, not a broken link. All image, video, and caption media URLs return 200/206 with the correct content type.

    Are product ratings or review counts included?

    Only when Amazon shows them on the storefront card. The current storefront markup does not display ratings on Idea List product cards, so none are fabricated.

    Limits and notes

    Limitations

    • The profile page's curation and trending-pick carousels expose up to 16 items each; that is the source ceiling.
    • Trending-pick cards carry ASIN, image, price, and badge, but no product title.
    • Photo posts expose the image and URL; their individually tagged products are not expanded.
    • Curations expose the title, post count, cover image, and URL; the posts inside a curation are not expanded.
    • Amazon occasionally soft-redirects an unprimed deep link to an unrelated page. The actor primes each session on the marketplace homepage and retries, but a persistent block is reported as BLOCKED rather than fabricated data.
    • updatedText is Amazon's relative text ("Updated 5 days ago"), not an absolute timestamp.

    Is this affiliated with Amazon?

    No — this is an independent third-party actor that reads Amazon's public storefront pages.

    Acquire.com Public Startup Listing Financials Extractor is an Apify Actor published by Nimbryxo Studio. It accepts 43 input fields, none of them required at the top level. Its build declares no dataset fields; the published README describes the output. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Acquire.com is a marketplace where founders list SaaS products, ecommerce stores, mobile apps, agencies, newsletters and other online businesses for sale. This actor turns those public listings into structured deal data: asking price, trailing-twelve-month revenue and profit, last month's numbers, valuation multiples, growth, customer metrics, company details and the seller's own acquisition notes. It reads only the pages Acquire publishes for search engines, so no account, cookie or proxy setup is required.

    What this actor does

    • Three ways to collect listings: browse the whole public marketplace, search it by keyword, or refresh a list of specific listing URLs
    • Full public financials: asking price, TTM and last-month revenue/profit, revenue and profit multiples, ARR, growth, customers, churn and other disclosed metrics
    • Deal filters: price, revenue, profit, multiples, growth, ARR, customers, business age, team size, category, location, business model, tech stack, financing, document counts, verified-business, recast-financials, open-to-offers and badge signals
    • Optional public Firebase enrichment: turn on includePublicDetails to add marketplace status and history, first-listed date, financial snapshot, recast SDE, Google Analytics traffic, views/favorites/conversations, file-count breakdown and seller flags
    • Covers the public marketplace: the sitemap currently exposes roughly 1,250 public listings, and a single run can return all of them
    • Clean records: empty fields are omitted at every depth, and numeric values are parsed from the display text so they can be sorted and charted
    • Legacy URLs keep working: both current /public/<slug> links and older /startup/<slug> links are accepted

    Use cases

    • Deal sourcing: build a shortlist of acquisition targets by category, price, revenue, profit, growth and multiple
    • Valuation research: compare revenue and profit multiples across SaaS, ecommerce, mobile and content businesses
    • Daily monitoring: schedule a newest-first run and send new listings to a spreadsheet, CRM, Slack or email
    • Due-diligence prep: pull the seller's description, price reasoning, highlights, assets, financing and document counts before an introductory call
    • Market mapping: see which tech stacks, business models and locations are coming to market
    • Portfolio screening: filter for recurring revenue, ARR, low churn, team size or a specific acquisition budget

    Questions

    Do I need an Acquire.com account?

    No. The actor reads only the public listing pages that Acquire exposes to search engines.

    Can I get the seller's identity, website, contact details or data-room files?

    No. Those remain restricted. Public enrichment adds aggregate seller flags and listing metadata, never seller IDs, names, emails, contacts or private document bytes.

    What does includePublicDetails add?

    For returned listings only, it reads Acquire's public Firebase documents and adds marketplace status/history, first-listed and update dates, financial snapshot, recast SDE, weekly Google Analytics traffic with channels, views/favorites/conversations, a file-count breakdown and public seller flags. The page parser remains the primary source; enrichment failures are skipped gracefully.

    Why are some fields missing for some listings?

    Sellers choose which metrics to disclose. A field appears only when the public page or, with includePublicDetails, the public Firebase document shows it; empty fields are omitted rather than filled with placeholders.

    Are the financial values numbers or text?

    The actor parses displayed values such as $1.2M, $351k and +974% into numbers, while keeping the original asking-price text in askingPriceText.

    How many listings can one run return?

    The public sitemap currently contains about 1,250 listings, and maxItems allows up to 1,250, so the whole public marketplace fits in one run.

    What does "newest" mean?

    It means most recently updated according to the sitemap's last-modified timestamp, not the date the listing was first published.

    Why do customer counts sometimes look low?

    Acquire often shows a band such as 1,000-5,000. customers holds the lower bound, customersHigh the upper bound, customersRange the original text and customersValueType says whether the source is exact, a band or openEnded.

    Do I need a proxy or cookies?

    No. The actor connects directly by default and can fall back to a standard platform proxy if the source starts blocking requests.

    Is this affiliated with Acquire.com?

    No. This is an independent, third-party actor that reads publicly available listing pages.

    What happens when no listing matches?

    The run still succeeds, returns no records, and the status message explains how many pages were inspected and why nothing matched.

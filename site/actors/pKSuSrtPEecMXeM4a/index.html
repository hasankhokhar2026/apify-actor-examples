Actors/Yahoo Finance Market Data & Fundamentals Scraper

Yahoo Finance Market Data & Fundamentals Scraper

Pull live quotes, historical OHLCV candles, financial statements, dividends, splits, news, analyst ratings and holder data from Yahoo Finance for any ticker, ETF, index, currency pair or crypto symbol.

Open on ApifyGitHub source

Input schema

Input fields, types, required flags, descriptions, defaults, and prefills come from the published definition. Prefill examples are distinct from defaults.

FieldTypeRequiredDescription

searchQuerystringoptionalCompany, fund, index, currency or crypto name to search for (mode=search).

Yahoo Finance Market Data & Fundamentals Scraper

Yahoo Finance is the default reference point for public market data, but its quote pages mix delayed prices, statements, analyst views, holder tables and option chains across dozens of separate views. This actor turns those views into one clean dataset: give it a watchlist, a search phrase, a screener, a region or nothing at all for a market overview, and receive structured records with source URLs and timestamps. It works for stocks, ETFs, mutual funds, indices, currency pairs, futures and crypto, and needs no account, API key or proxy setup.

What this actor does

  • Six modes: tickers (default), search, screener, customScreener, trending, marketSummary
  • Deep quote data: regular, pre-market and US post-market prices, bid/ask, day and 52-week ranges, valuation ratios, margins, growth, short interest, analyst targets and company risk scores
  • History and events: 13 chart intervals, 11 ranges, optional pre/post-market bars, adjusted close, full dividend history and split history
  • Statements and ownership: annual and quarterly income, balance-sheet and cash-flow statements with expanded high-fill fields; institutional, fund, major-holder and insider data
  • Options and funds: multiple current expirations with contract-level filters; ETF/mutual-fund profile, top holdings, bond ratings, sector weights, Morningstar ratings and performance
  • Research extras: search results with a localized lookup fallback, 22 predefined screeners, a region-aware custom screener, region-based trending symbols, market summary, up to 200 news items, analyst ratings, SEC filings and related symbols
  • Empty fields are omitted — optional data that Yahoo does not publish for a symbol is left out rather than emitted as null, "" or []

Known hard limits

These are limits of Yahoo's public data, verified during the revamp; the actor does not fabricate values for them:

  • Options history and source greeks: only current option chains are served, and there are no source greeks (no delta/gamma/theta/vega in Yahoo's contract payload). Historical chains are unavailable.
  • US-only options: option chains exist only for optionable US-listed symbols; no HK/EU/index/FX/crypto chains.
  • Statements for funds, indices, FX, crypto and futures: Yahoo has no income, balance-sheet or cash-flow statements for these instrument types. Fund profiles and holdings are the substitute.
  • ESG/sustainability: Yahoo retired the ESG modules and page; the field is not emitted.
  • Predefined screener region: the predefined endpoint is US-centric and ignores region; use customScreener.
  • CJK direct search: /v1/finance/search rejects non-Latin text with HTTP 400; the localized /v1/finance/lookup fallback is the supported path and can still return zero documents.
  • Screener page size: Yahoo accepts at most 250 rows per request; the actor paginates to reach maxResults.
  • News source cap: the graph is paginated by cursor and is capped at 200 items per ticker by this actor; the search fallback is smaller.
  • Trending sparsity: several regions return only 1–4 symbols upstream.

FAQ

Do I need an API key, login, cookies or a proxy? No. The actor reads Yahoo Finance's public web data without an account. It uses no user cookies or API keys, and it works without a proxy by default.

Are quotes real-time? Most Yahoo quotes are delayed, often by about 15 minutes for US equities. The record's quoteSource, exchangeDataDelayedBy and marketState fields tell you what Yahoo reported for that snapshot.

Can I get pre-market and after-hours prices? Pre-market fields are included when Yahoo publishes them. postMarket* fields (postMarketPrice, postMarketChange, postMarketChangePercent, postMarketTime) are mapped from Yahoo's quote summary (with the quote-v7 fields as fallback). Post-market values were verified for US-listed equities and ETFs (12/12) and were absent for non-US listings, indices, FX, crypto and futures (0/18), so treat these fields as US-only. Intraday history can include pre/post-market bars with includePrePost.

Why are some fields missing for my ticker? Not every instrument type has every data type. Indices, currencies, futures and crypto usually have quotes and charts but no company statements, holders or options. ETFs have fund data instead of company statements. The actor omits a field when Yahoo has no value for it.

How many news items can I get? The actor reads Yahoo's Finance Nexus graph first and paginates its cursor, so maxNews supports up to 200 items per symbol (AAPL returned 148, MSFT 132, NVDA 182 and SPY 138 in verification). News works for equities, ETFs, indices, FX and crypto on this path. If the graph returns nothing, the actor falls back to Yahoo's search feed (about 50 items) and then the HTML news page. Results are deduplicated by URL; news can mention related tickers, so use relatedTickers when you need to filter relevance.

Why is the options chain missing? Options exist only for optionable US-listed symbols. Foreign listings, indices, currencies, futures and crypto do not have a Yahoo option chain. Yahoo exposes only current expirations, not historical chains, and its contract payload contains no delta, gamma, theta or vega. Historical options, source-provided greeks and non-US option chains are confirmed hard limits; the actor does not invent them.

Why are quarterly statements missing? Quarterly statements require both includeFinancials and includeQuarterlyFinancials. Operating companies, not funds, indices, FX, crypto or futures, publish them; those instrument types have no Yahoo income/balance/cash-flow statements (a confirmed hard limit). Use includeFundData for fund profiles and holdings. Some foreign issuers report only annual periods.

What does financials contain? financials.income, financials.balance and financials.cashflow are keyed by fiscal period. Values are in the reporting currency shown in financials.currency; financials.periods lists the available dates. Quarterly groups sit under financials.quarterly. Item keys keep the legacy underscore labels such as Total_Revenue, Net_Income and Operating_Cash_Flow, so existing spreadsheet and API integrations continue to work.

Why is dividendYield a small number like 0.31? For backward compatibility, dividendYield keeps Yahoo's percentage number: 0.31 means 0.31%. The additive trailingAnnualDividendYield is a fraction: 0.0031 means the same yield. heldPercentInsiders and heldPercentInstitutions are fractions; debtToEquity is a percentage number.

Why is the sustainability/ESG field gone? Yahoo retired its ESG endpoints and the sustainability page no longer returns scores. The actor does not advertise or emit a sustainability field, and an old task that sends includeSustainability continues to run with a logged no-op.

Can I search in any language? Company names and news localize through the language and region inputs, and unicode names are preserved in output. Yahoo's /v1/finance/search endpoint rejects non-Latin text with HTTP 400 (a confirmed source limit), so for Japanese, Chinese or Korean queries the actor automatically falls back to /v1/finance/lookup with the selected language and region. Lookup returned documents for most JA/ZH-Hans/ZH-Hant/KO probes. If it returns none, the run status says so explicitly; try a ticker (7203.T), an English name, or another region/language.

How are fund and option values expressed? Fund positions (cashPosition, stockPosition, holdingPercent) are fractions: 0.0836 means 8.36%. fundProfile.totalNetAssets is absolute fund assets (USD for US-listed funds), normalized from Yahoo's raw millions when that is the only value available. Option impliedVolatility uses Yahoo's percentage number: 2.0859 means 208.59%, while the minImpliedVolatilityPercent input is a percent: 25 means 25%. marketSummary.dayChangePercent is also Yahoo's percentage number. postMarketChangePercent is normalized to the same percentage-number convention (0.63 means 0.63%). Statement values are in financials.currency.

Why do predefined screeners return US rows even when I pick another region? Yahoo's predefined screener endpoint is US-centric and ignores the region parameter (confirmed hard limit). Use mode: "customScreener" instead: it posts a region operand to Yahoo's custom screener and returned regional rows for 30 of 31 region codes (JP 3,814; IN 8,553; PE 0). The actor paginates in 250-row requests until maxResults or source exhaustion. Predefined screeners are unchanged.

Why does trending return only a few symbols for some regions? Yahoo's trending feed is sparse outside the US. A region with one to three symbols is a genuine upstream result, not an early stop. The run status reports the shortfall, and mode: "customScreener" provides a region-wide fallback.

What happens to a delisted or invalid symbol? The actor emits an explicit error record with reason: "no_data" and continues with the other symbols. It does not emit a silent partial ticker record. Aliases such as BRK.B are resolved to Yahoo's spelling (BRK-B) and return data. Rare-symbol HTTP 500 quote pages were not reproduced locally (306 of 306 rare/crypto pages returned 200); the actor still keeps the quote-v7, chart and HTML fallbacks and reports an error rather than fabricating values.

Why are some fund fields missing? topHoldings.bondRatings and topHoldings.sectorWeightings are present when Yahoo publishes them; equity sector weights are empty for non-equity funds such as GLD and TLT. fundProfile.totalNetAssets is normalized to absolute assets and omitted when Yahoo only returns a zero sentinel; fundProfile.inceptionDate comes from Yahoo's fund inception field, not the current manager's start date, and is omitted when unavailable. Morningstar overall/risk ratings are populated for mutual funds but empty for most ETFs, so they are omitted when absent rather than emitted as zero. SEC filings can be attached to one share class only; for example GOOGL's secFilings module is empty while GOOG returns filings, so the actor falls back to the sibling class and records secFilingsSourceSymbol.

Is this affiliated with Yahoo? No. This is an independent, third-party actor that reads Yahoo Finance's publicly available data and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Yahoo.

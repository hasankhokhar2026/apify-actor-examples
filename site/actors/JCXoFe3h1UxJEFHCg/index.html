Indeed Global Job Scraper with Company Salary Reports

Search Indeed jobs across 62 countries by keyword, location, or filters for job type, remote status, experience, education, and salary. Returns full descriptions, structured salaries, and each employer's ratings and salary-by-title reports.

Input fields, types, required flags, descriptions, defaults, and prefills come from the published definition. Prefill examples are distinct from defaults.

positionstringoptionalJob title, skill or keywords, e.g. `web developer`, `nurse`. Search one company's jobs with `company:Amazon`, titles only with `title:(data analyst)`; quotes, OR and -exclusions work too.
locationstringoptionalCity, region or postal code, e.g. `San Francisco`, `Austin, TX`, `10001`, `London`. Leave empty to search the whole country.
companystringoptionalOnly jobs from one employer, e.g. `Amazon`, `Visa`, `DocuSign`. The name is resolved to Indeed's employer key so the company filter is applied by Indeed itself, then verified per job.

jobTypestringoptionalEmployment type.
workplaceTypestringoptionalWhere the work happens.
datePostedstringoptionalOnly jobs added to Indeed within this period.
experienceLevelstringoptionalRequired experience level.
educationLevelstringoptionalRequired education.
salaryMinintegeroptionalOnly jobs whose salary range reaches at least this yearly amount, in the country's currency (hourly and monthly pay is converted to yearly). Jobs without any salary information are excluded.
easyApplyOnlybooleanoptionalOnly jobs you can apply to directly on Indeed.
radiusintegeroptionalDistance around the location (Indeed's default is 25). Ignored without a location.
radiusUnitstringoptionalUnit of the search radius.
sortstringoptionalOrder of the search results.
startUrlsarrayoptionalIndeed search result URLs (any country, with their filters), job URLs (`…/viewjob?jk=…`) or job IDs. Each search URL is scraped as its own search; job URLs are fetched directly. Filters chosen above are added to search URLs.

includeDescriptionHtmlbooleanoptionalAlso save the job description as HTML (`descriptionHtml`) with its formatting. The plain-text `description` is always included.
saveOnlyUniqueItemsbooleanoptionalSave each job once even when several searches find it. Jobs are always unique within one search.

Indeed Global Job Scraper with Company Salary Reports

Scrape Indeed jobs from 62 country sites by keyword, location, company and Indeed's own filters. Every row includes the full description, normalized salary, location with coordinates, benefits, recruiter contacts, freshness signals and the employer's Indeed rating report — with no Indeed account, login, cookies or proxy setup.

What this actor does

  • Search, URLs and filters: search by position, location and company, or paste Indeed search URLs, company job pages, job URLs and bare job IDs; apply date, salary, job type, workplace, easy apply, experience, education, radius and sort, with every result re-checked before saving
  • Salary that compares: hourly, daily, weekly and monthly pay is converted to a yearly figure, with the original text, currency, period and source kept
  • Company report in the same row: app-API ratings (overall, five category ratings, review count) always; optional HTML report adds CEO approval, founding year, wellbeing scores, industry fallback and average pay by job title
  • Freshness and coverage signals: displayTitle, refNum, isRepost, isLatestPost, isSponsored and retrieved-vs-reported counts so a short run is visible instead of silent
  • Empty fields are omitted: a value Indeed does not have for a job is left out of that row, never replaced with null or a guess

FAQ

How many jobs can I get from one search? Indeed's cursor returns roughly 1,000 fetched rows and then stops, including duplicates; the unique yield is usually lower. maxItemsPerSearch is a save cap, not a promise that Indeed has that many reachable rows. To go deeper, add locationShards or separate search URLs and keep deduplication on. The run reports searchFacetTotal, searchRetrieved and searchCoverage; the facet number is an upper bound, not the reachable count.

Why is searchFacetTotal much larger than what I received? It is Indeed's own count for the search, and its API exposes only the first cursor window of that set. A search reporting 100,000 matches may still return only a few hundred unique jobs. The actor states this in the run status instead of presenting the facet count as delivered data. Per-row coverage is a save-time snapshot, except the last row of each search, which carries the final retrieved count and coverage.

Why do some jobs have no salary? Many employers do not publish pay on Indeed. Salary fields appear only when the employer publishes them, and coverage varies by country: the United States and United Kingdom are usually well covered, Germany much less. salaryAnnualMin and salaryAnnualMax convert hourly, daily, weekly and monthly pay to a yearly figure. Indeed's own estimated salary is not returned because the app API does not populate it.

How does the minimum salary filter work? Indeed filters natively on the annual salary field, but it also keeps jobs with no salary at all. The actor then re-checks every job and keeps only those whose published range reaches your minimum. Jobs without salary data are excluded when salaryMin is set.

How do I get all jobs from one company? Use the company input, or search company:CompanyName as the position, or paste the company's Indeed jobs page URL. The actor resolves the company name to Indeed's employer key so the filter is native, then verifies the employer on each saved job.

Does it extract contact details? Emails and phone numbers that the employer wrote directly into the job description are saved in emails and phoneNumbers (international format). Most employers ask candidates to apply through Indeed or their careers site, so only a minority of jobs contain contact details this way.

Which countries are supported? 62 Indeed sites, including the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, India, Germany, France, Japan, Brazil and Mexico. Results come back in the local language with salaries in the local currency, and the locale is pinned to the job's country so salary data is not dropped.

Why is isSponsored missing on some rows? Sponsored placement is not part of Indeed's app job API. The actor reads it from the mobile results page, where about 47 jobs are visible per request; a job outside that window keeps the field omitted rather than guessed. Turn includeSponsored off for the cheapest run.

What happens if a pasted job URL is expired or removed? The actor first retries the job API, then tries Indeed's mobile job page. If both fail, it saves an error row (recordType: "error") with the job key, URL and reason, so the dataset tells you what happened instead of silently dropping the URL.

Is this affiliated with Indeed? No. This is an independent, third-party actor that reads publicly available job listings. It is not operated by or affiliated with Indeed.

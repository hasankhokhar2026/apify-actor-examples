    Wish.com Product Price & Rating Scraper is an Apify Actor published by Nimbryxo Studio. It accepts 9 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 8 declared fields, including name, price, salePrice, rating, ratingCount and merchantName. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Wish.com lists budget, direct-from-manufacturer products across dozens of categories, each with its own price, discount, rating, and merchant information. This actor queries Wish by keyword, category, trending list, or specific product ID and returns one structured record per product.

    What this actor does

    • Searches Wish by keyword, browses a category, fetches the trending/featured list, or looks up specific products by ID.
    • Paginates automatically to collect up to the number of products you request.
    • Filters search/byCategory/trending results by price range, minimum rating, and sale status.
    • Returns price, rating, and merchant ID from search/category/trending listings; byProductId lookups return a lighter record (see below).
    • Fields Wish doesn't provide for a given product are left out of that product's record.

    Use cases

    • Dropshipping research — scan a category or keyword for low-priced, well-rated products worth sourcing.
    • Price monitoring — track price for a set of search results over time.
    • Trend spotting — pull the trending list on a schedule to see what's currently being promoted.
    • Catalog building — collect product images and listing prices for a category to seed a comparison feed.

    byProductId returns a lighter record from the product detail page — productId, productUrl, mainImageUrl (when found), plus the same recordType/siteName/scrapedAt — without name, price, rating, or merchantId. isOnSale on a byProductId record is always false, since the detail page lookup doesn't carry sale-price data; set onSaleOnly: true only with search/byCategory/trending, since it will exclude every byProductId result.

    Questions

    Do I need a Wish account to use this?

    No. This actor reads publicly accessible Wish product pages and listings.

    What categories can I browse?

    byCategory accepts any of 20 categories covering fashion, electronics, home & garden, beauty, toys, sports, automotive, pets, jewelry, health, bags & accessories, office & school, tools, baby, gaming, outdoor, shoes, phone accessories, computers, and kitchen.

    Why are some fields missing on certain products?

    Not every product tile exposes every field, and byProductId lookups return a smaller field set than search/byCategory/trending (see Output above). Fields that Wish doesn't provide for a given product, or that this actor doesn't extract for that mode, are omitted rather than filled with a placeholder.

    What is a Wish product ID, and where do I find one?

    It's the 24-character hex string that appears in a product's URL (wish.com/product/{productId}). Any product returned by search, byCategory, or trending includes its own productId you can reuse with byProductId.

    How current is rating?

    It reflects whatever Wish is currently displaying on the search/category/trending listing tile at the time of the run.

    Can I combine minPrice, maxPrice, minRating, and onSaleOnly in one run?

    Yes, but they only have an effect on search, byCategory, and trending results — byProductId records don't carry price/rating/sale data, so onSaleOnly: true will exclude every byProductId result.

    Is this affiliated with Wish?

    No. This actor is an independent tool that reads Wish's public product pages and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Wish (ContextLogic Inc.).

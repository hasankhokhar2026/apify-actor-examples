    ImportYeti Importer & Supplier Trade Explorer is an Apify Actor published by Nimbryxo Studio. It accepts 15 input fields, and mode is required. Its build declares no dataset fields; the published README describes the output. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    ImportYeti publishes US ocean-import records: who imported what, which overseas company shipped it, and the shipment volume behind the relationship. This actor turns those public pages into one structured dataset you can search, filter and join.

    It works without a login, cookie, API key or browser. ImportYeti's public pages are read directly, and the actor rotates its network session automatically when the site's pageview limit is reached.

    Modes

    Data source and compliance

    The actor reads only public ImportYeti pages: company and supplier profiles, search results, HS-code pages, location directories and product tags. It does not access a logged-in account, premium data, private records or a paid API. Shipment records originate in US Customs bill-of-lading filings. Use the data lawfully and respect the source site's terms.

    Use cases

    • Find the overseas companies shipping a product category to the US.
    • Map a competitor's top suppliers and recent import activity.
    • Build a list of US importers for an HS code or a location.
    • Check a partner's shipment recency, volume and trade lanes before outreach.
    • Research HS/HTS codes, carriers and port pairs behind a company's imports.

    Output

    Every record starts with the same envelope: type (importyeti_record), recordKind, name, recordType, countryCode, address, detailUrl, matchSource, matchValue, sourcePageUrl and scrapedAt.

    recordKind tells you what the row is:

    • profile — one company or supplier, from a search hit or a company/supplier URL.
    • tradeSummary — one HS code, location or product tag, with totals and its nested partner/shipment tables.
    • partner — one row from an HS/location/tag supplier or company table.

    Key fields include:

    • Identity — name, recordType, alsoKnownAs, website, otherWebsites, address, addressParsed, addressPlain, locationHierarchy, latitude, longitude.
    • Shipment summary — totalShipments, shipments12m, mostRecentShipment, mostRecentShipmentDate, firstShipmentDate, dateRange, totalShippingCost, shippingCostCoverage, avgTeuPerShipment, avgTeuPerMonth, quarterlyGrowth.
    • Partners — topSuppliers (names) and topPartners (structured rows with role, address, country, shipments, weight, TEU, HS codes and counterparties).
    • Recent shipments — recentShipments with date, BOL/master/house numbers, counterparty, product description, weight, quantity, containers, route and estimated freight cost.
    • Product codes — hsCodes, htsCodes, topImports.
    • Logistics — shippingLanes, topCarriers, carriersPerCountry, scacCodesDiversity, containers, containerTypes, containerLoad.
    • Other — notifyParties, otherAddresses, tags, trademarks, trademarkCount, monthlyShipments, originCountries, chartImages, uflpa, uflpaUrl, manifestConfidential.
    • Provenance — matchSource, matchValue, sourcePageUrl, scrapedAt.

    Empty values are omitted; the actor never pushes null, empty strings or placeholder text.

    Questions

    Do I need an ImportYeti account or API key?

    No. The actor reads public pages only and needs no login, cookie or key.

    Why did I get fewer rows than maxResults?

    The source may have fewer matches, or the query hit the 280-row search ceiling. Add another query, use an HS/location/tag mode, or lower the filters.

    Why are phone numbers and emails missing?

    ImportYeti redacts them for visitors who are not logged in. The actor omits masked contact fields rather than guessing.

    Why are there only 50 shipments for a large importer?

    ImportYeti publishes the 50 most recent bills of lading per company or supplier. Older shipment detail is not on the public page.

    How are suppliers and importers distinguished?

    ImportYeti calls US importers company and overseas exporters supplier. Use entityType to filter, and read recordType on every row.

    Does this use a browser or proxy?

    No browser is involved. The actor uses normal HTTPS requests and selects its network session automatically. You do not configure a proxy.

    Is this affiliated with ImportYeti?

    No. This is an independent tool. US customs bill-of-lading data is public; users are responsible for complying with applicable law and the source site's terms.

    Limits and notes

    What you can do

    • Search by name, product or address — match importers, suppliers and indexed product text.
    • Paste ImportYeti URLs — company, supplier, HS-code, location, product-tag or search links.
    • Look up an HS code — get the code summary, its top suppliers, top US importers and recent shipments.
    • Browse a location — a country, state, county or city directory of suppliers or importers.
    • Browse a product tag — see the companies and suppliers behind a product category.
    • Open profiles — add top trading partners, recent bills of lading, HS/HTS codes, port lanes, carriers, containers, notify parties, other addresses, tags and UFLPA flags.

    Limits you should know

    • Search reach: ImportYeti's public search serves up to 14 pages of 10 results for one entity type — 140 rows per role, 280 per query when both types are fetched. totalHits and totalPages in the source JSON describe the whole index, not what is reachable. Use more searchQueries to cover more.
    • Profile reach: each public profile exposes at most 50 top partners and 50 recent bills of lading. Older individual shipments are not public.
    • Pageview limit: ImportYeti allows about 25 pageviews per IP address, shared by search and profile pages. The actor rotates its network session automatically; if every rung is blocked, it fails honestly instead of returning an empty success.
    • Contacts: ImportYeti masks phone numbers and email addresses for anonymous visitors. Those fields are not returned.
    • Source quirks: some partner names are filed as Missing in source document; those rows are omitted. Website values occasionally arrive truncated by the source; invalid domains are omitted.

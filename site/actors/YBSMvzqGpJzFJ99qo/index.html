LinkedIn Job Listings with Salary & Company Data Scraper

Collect LinkedIn job postings by keyword, location, or search URL, with parsed salary ranges, visa-sponsorship signals, and enriched employer profiles. No login required.

Open on ApifyGitHub source

Input schema

Input fields, types, required flags, descriptions, defaults, and prefills come from the published definition. Prefill examples are distinct from defaults.

FieldTypeRequiredDescription
searchQueriesarrayoptionalJob titles, skills or phrases to search for, one per line (e.g. 'data analyst', 'registered nurse'). Each keyword is searched in every location you list, and results are merged without duplicates.
searchUrlsarrayoptionalPaste job search links copied from LinkedIn (linkedin.com/jobs/search/?...). The keywords, location and filters inside each link are kept.
jobUrlsarrayoptionalScrape specific job postings: paste job links (linkedin.com/jobs/view/...) or numeric job IDs. Postings that have been taken down are skipped.

FieldTypeRequiredDescription
searchQueriesarrayoptionalJob titles, skills or phrases to search for, one per line (e.g. 'data analyst', 'registered nurse'). Each keyword is searched in every location you list, and results are merged without duplicates.
searchUrlsarrayoptionalPaste job search links copied from LinkedIn (linkedin.com/jobs/search/?...). The keywords, location and filters inside each link are kept.
jobUrlsarrayoptionalScrape specific job postings: paste job links (linkedin.com/jobs/view/...) or numeric job IDs. Postings that have been taken down are skipped.
locationsarrayoptionalCities, regions or countries in English (e.g. 'Germany', 'Austin, Texas', 'London Area, United Kingdom'). Every keyword is searched in each location. A location LinkedIn doesn't recognise is reported and skipped rather than silently searched as the United States. Leave empty to search worldwide.
geoIdsarrayoptionalOptional numeric LinkedIn location IDs (the geoId value in a LinkedIn search link, e.g. 101282230 for Germany). Use these for exact places in any language.
distancestringoptionalSearch radius around a city-level location, in miles.
timePostedstringoptionalOnly jobs posted (or reposted) within this period.
companiesarrayoptionalOnly jobs from these employers. Enter company names (e.g. 'General Motors'), LinkedIn company or university page links, or numeric company IDs. Leave the keywords empty to get all of a company's open jobs.
excludeCompaniesarrayoptionalSkip jobs from these employers (exact company name or LinkedIn company page link).
easyApplybooleanoptionalOnly jobs you can apply to directly on LinkedIn.
under10ApplicantsbooleanoptionalOnly jobs that have had fewer than 10 applicants so far.
experienceLevelstringoptionalOnly jobs listed at this seniority level on LinkedIn. Jobs that LinkedIn lists as 'Not Applicable' are left out when a level is chosen.
jobTypestringoptionalOnly jobs with this employment type.
industryarrayoptionalOnly jobs from employers in these LinkedIn industries.
companySizesarrayoptionalOnly jobs from employers of these sizes, as listed on their LinkedIn page. Each employer's page is looked up once. Jobs whose employer size isn't listed are kept.
excludeStaffingAgenciesbooleanoptionalSkip jobs posted by employers in LinkedIn's 'Staffing and Recruiting' industry.
requireSalarybooleanoptionalOnly jobs that state their pay, either in LinkedIn's pay range box or in the job description.
minYearlySalaryintegeroptionalSkip jobs whose stated pay range tops out below this yearly amount. Hourly, weekly and monthly pay is converted to a yearly figure. The amount is compared in the job's own currency, so set 'Salary currency' to compare like with like. Jobs without a stated salary are kept unless 'Only jobs with a salary' is on.
salaryCurrencystringoptionalOnly jobs whose stated pay is in this currency. Also sets the currency of 'Minimum yearly salary'. Jobs without a stated salary are kept unless 'Only jobs with a salary' is on.
requireRecruiterbooleanoptionalOnly jobs where LinkedIn shows the person who posted the job.
includeTitleKeywordsarrayoptionalKeep only jobs whose title contains at least one of these words (not case-sensitive). Useful because LinkedIn's keyword matching is broad.
excludeTitleKeywordsarrayoptionalSkip jobs whose title contains any of these words, e.g. 'Senior' or 'Intern'.
excludeJobIdsarrayoptionalJob IDs (the 'id' field of an earlier run) to leave out, so scheduled runs return only new postings.
scrapeJobDetailsbooleanoptionalOpen each job to add the full description, salary, seniority, job type, industry, applicant count, recruiter and Easy Apply status. Turn off for a fast list of titles, companies, locations and dates only. Filters that need these details switch it on automatically.
includeCompanyDetailsbooleanoptionalAdd each employer's LinkedIn profile: industry, size, headquarters, website, founding year, office locations, followers, tagline and description. Fetched once per company.
enrichmentDepthstringoptionalStandard returns each job's core details. Extended also adds similar jobs, 'people also viewed' jobs, related searches and LinkedIn's numeric industry and title IDs, and asks LinkedIn again when the exact posting time is missing, so it takes longer per job.
languagestringoptionalLanguage LinkedIn uses for labels such as job type and seniority. Job descriptions stay in the language they were written in. Locations must still be typed in English.
splitLargeSearchesbooleanoptionalLinkedIn returns at most 1,000 jobs for one search. When a search reaches that limit, continue with the same search limited to the past month, week, 3 days and 24 hours, which surfaces many more jobs (duplicates removed).
maxItemsintegeroptionalStop after saving this many jobs. A single keyword + location search returns at most 1,000 jobs; add keywords or locations to collect more.

LinkedIn Job Listings with Salary & Company Data Scraper

Turn LinkedIn's public job board into a clean spreadsheet. Search by keywords, locations and employers, or paste job links, and get one row per posting with the full description, a yearly salary range with its source, applicant numbers, Easy Apply status, seniority, the recruiter when LinkedIn shows one, and the employer's LinkedIn profile. No LinkedIn account, login or cookies needed.

What this actor does

  • Salary you can sort by. Pay is parsed into minimum, maximum, currency and period, then converted to yearly figures. It comes from LinkedIn's own pay box or, when the employer only wrote it in the description, from that text. salarySource tells you which.
  • Many searches in one run. Several keywords times several locations, company-only searches, pasted LinkedIn search links and individual job links, merged without duplicates.
  • Filters that really narrow the results. Date posted (from the past hour to the past 3 months), distance, Easy Apply, fewer than 10 applicants, companies to include or exclude, experience level, job type, industry, staffing agencies, salary and recruiter.
  • Employer profile included. Industry, size, headquarters, website, founding year, office addresses, follower count, tagline and description, looked up once per company.
  • Empty fields are omitted. Fields LinkedIn doesn't show for a posting are left out instead of filled with blanks or guesses.

Output per job

Field What it contains
id, title, jobUrl LinkedIn job ID, job title and link
companyName, companyUrl, companyId, companyNumericId, companyLogo Employer name, LinkedIn page, page name, numeric company ID and logo
location Location as LinkedIn shows it

FAQ

Do I need a LinkedIn account or cookies? No. The actor reads the job pages LinkedIn shows to signed-out visitors.

Can I filter for remote, hybrid or on-site jobs? Neither the public search nor the job page shows the remote/hybrid/on-site label to signed-out visitors, and the search ignores the workplace filter, so a remote filter can't be offered. remoteAllowed is true when the title or description clearly says the role is fully remote, or when the job page includes LinkedIn's structured data marking it TELECOMMUTE. That structured block only ever marks remote jobs, never hybrid or on-site, so neither is inferred; not every remote job carries it, so the field can be missing for a remote role. Adding "remote" to your keywords narrows results to jobs that mention it. Older saved inputs that still contain the remote filter or the job-function filter keep running; those two settings are ignored and the run's status message says so.

Why do some jobs have no salary? Many employers don't publish pay. The actor reads LinkedIn's pay box and pay ranges written in the description, and never estimates. salarySource shows where each figure came from. Expect most US jobs in pay-transparency states to have a salary, and far fewer elsewhere.

How do I get more than 1,000 jobs? LinkedIn returns at most 1,000 jobs for one search. When a search reaches that limit, the actor automatically continues with the same search restricted to the past month, week, 3 days and 24 hours. Each of those returns its own results, and duplicates are removed, so one keyword and location can yield well over 1,000 jobs. For even more, add locations, keywords or companies. Turn this off with splitLargeSearches if you only want LinkedIn's top 1,000.

Why can't I get the employer's own application link? LinkedIn only shows the external "Apply on company website" link to signed-in members. applyUrl is the LinkedIn page where the application starts, and easyApply tells you whether you can apply on LinkedIn directly.

My location returned the wrong country. LinkedIn quietly searches the whole United States when it doesn't recognise a place. The actor checks every location first: an unrecognised one is matched to LinkedIn's closest suggestion or skipped with a note in the status message. Type places in English ("Germany", not "Deutschland"), or use geoIds.

Which details does LinkedIn hide from signed-out visitors? Remote/hybrid/on-site labels, the employer's own application link, whether a job was reposted, view counts and the job-function category are only shown to signed-in members, so this actor can't return them. Expiry dates are partly available: when the job page carries structured data, expiresAt is LinkedIn's own expiry time. Workarounds: add "remote" to your keywords or use includeTitleKeywords with "remote" or "hybrid"; use remoteAllowed for jobs that clearly state they're fully remote or whose structured data marks them TELECOMMUTE; use postedDate (the latest posting date) for freshness; to track reposts and closures yourself, run on a schedule and compare id values between runs. The exact posting time (postedAt) appears only on some versions of a job page. Extended job page depth asks again to fill it for most jobs, but not all; postedDate is always filled.

How do I get only new jobs on a schedule? Use "Date posted: Past 24 hours", or keep the id values from earlier runs and pass them in excludeJobIds.

Do I need to set up a proxy? No. The actor picks its connection automatically and switches when LinkedIn slows it down.

Is this affiliated with LinkedIn? No. It is an independent tool that reads LinkedIn's public job pages.

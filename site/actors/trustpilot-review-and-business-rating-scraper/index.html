    Trustpilot Review & Business Rating Scraper is an Apify Actor published by Nimbryxo Studio. It accepts 13 input fields, and businessDomains, mode and proxyConfiguration are required. Each result can carry up to 9 declared fields, including businessDomain, rating, title, body, reviewerName and reviewerCountry. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Trustpilot hosts consumer reviews for millions of businesses, each with its own TrustScore and rating distribution. This actor reads any number of business review pages in one run and returns either every individual review or a per-business rating summary, with rating, language, date, verification and reply filters applied before you see the data.

    What this actor does

    • Returns per-review records for any number of business domains in a single run
    • Or returns per-business summary records (TrustScore, total reviews, rating distribution, categories, claimed status)
    • Filters: minRating / maxRating, verifiedOnly, responseRequired, language, dateRangeFrom / dateRangeTo, containsKeyword
    • Collects up to 1,000 reviews per business
    • Requires a residential proxy, since Trustpilot blocks datacenter IPs behind a JS challenge — see the FAQ
    • No empty or null fields ever appear in records — only data that was actually found

    Use cases

    • Reputation management — daily monitor of new reviews and replies for tracked brands
    • Competitor research — bulk-summarize TrustScores + rating distributions across competitors
    • Brand sentiment tracking — keyword filter (e.g. delivery, cancel) for high-volume brands
    • Compliance / consumer protection — pull every 1-star review citing specific issues
    • Customer-success ROI — count replies-to-reviews ratio per business
    • Localization research — filter by language to compare sentiment across markets
    • Investor due diligence — track multi-month rating-distribution trends for a private brand
    • Journalism — bulk-export negative reviews for an investigative piece

    Questions

    Why is residential proxy required?

    Trustpilot's pages are protected by AWS WAF, which blocks datacenter IPs with a JS-challenge interstitial. Residential IPs pass the challenge transparently. The actor fails fast if no residential proxy is configured.

    Does it require login or cookies?

    No. All Trustpilot business pages are public.

    What's the difference between reviewedAt and experiencedAt?

    reviewedAt is when the consumer published the review on Trustpilot. experiencedAt is the service date the consumer reported (when they actually used the service). Both are extracted when present.

    Are reviewer profiles included?

    Yes — reviewerName, reviewerCountry, and reviewerId (when public). Some reviewers post anonymously and only the display name is available.

    Can I scrape replies-only?

    Yes — set responseRequired: true. Note: many high-volume retailers (Amazon, Walmart, etc.) don't reply to most recent reviews on Trustpilot, so this filter may return 0 results for big brands; smaller businesses with active customer-care typically yield more matches.

    What's the throughput?

    Trustpilot loads 20 reviews per page. Expect roughly 1,000 reviews per business within about a minute, including initial proxy session setup.

    Are old reviews available?

    Yes — Trustpilot keeps reviews dating back years. dateRangeFrom lets you filter the window. Heads-up: Trustpilot returns reviews newest-first and caps deep pagination at ~10-12 pages (~200-240 reviews) from many IPs, so a tight dateRangeFrom/dateRangeTo window targeting reviews older than the actor can reach may return 0 records on high-volume businesses. For the broadest result set, use dateRangeFrom alone (no upper bound) or run on a smaller, lower-traffic business.

    What's a "verified" review?

    Trustpilot flags a review as verified if it can confirm the reviewer used the service (e.g. via the company's review-invitation flow).

    Can I scrape Trustpilot consumer profiles?

    Not in v1 — only business pages are supported.

    Why don't I see a helpfulCount on every review?

    Trustpilot only surfaces helpful counts on reviews with at least one upvote. Reviews with zero helpful votes have helpfulCount: 0 (kept) or omit the field if Trustpilot doesn't expose it.

    Is this affiliated with Trustpilot?

    No, this actor is third-party and uses publicly accessible business pages.

    Seek.com.au Job Search with Salary & Work-Type Filters is an Apify Actor published by Nimbryxo Studio. It accepts 26 input fields, none of them required at the top level. Its build declares no dataset fields; the published README describes the output. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    The full README, with every mode, example and answer the publisher documents, is on the Apify Store page. Its sections: “What this actor does”, “Output per job”, “Input”, “Use cases” and “FAQ”.

    What you get

    The Actor saves one record per result to an Apify dataset. The build declares no dataset view; the README documents 17 fields. Fields without a value can be left out of a record.

    What it costs

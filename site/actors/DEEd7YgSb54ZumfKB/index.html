TikTok Verified Mention Tracker

Find TikTok posts that tag a specific creator or brand handle, with each result checked against TikTok's own mention markup so you can tell a real @mention from a caption that merely contains the name.

Open on ApifyGitHub source

Input schema

Input fields, types, required flags, descriptions, defaults, and prefills come from the published definition. Prefill examples are distinct from defaults.

FieldTypeRequiredDescription
usernamesarrayrequiredBrand or creator accounts whose mentions you want. Type a handle (nike), a handle with @ (@nike) or paste a profile link (https://www.tiktok.com/@nike). One account per line; duplicates are merged.
mentionFilterstringoptionalEvery saved post carries a mentionType label: 'tagged' (the caption contains TikTok's clickable tag of the account), 'captionText' (the caption types @handle without a working tag) or 'searchResult' (TikTok search returned it for the handle, but the caption doesn't name the account). By default you get real mentions: tagged posts plus @handle captions.
excludeOwnPostsbooleanoptionalLeave out posts published by the tracked account itself, so you only see what other people say about it.

FieldTypeRequiredDescription
usernamesarrayrequiredBrand or creator accounts whose mentions you want. Type a handle (nike), a handle with @ (@nike) or paste a profile link (https://www.tiktok.com/@nike). One account per line; duplicates are merged.
mentionFilterstringoptionalEvery saved post carries a mentionType label: 'tagged' (the caption contains TikTok's clickable tag of the account), 'captionText' (the caption types @handle without a working tag) or 'searchResult' (TikTok search returned it for the handle, but the caption doesn't name the account). By default you get real mentions: tagged posts plus @handle captions.
excludeOwnPostsbooleanoptionalLeave out posts published by the tracked account itself, so you only see what other people say about it.
datePostedstringoptionalOnly look at posts published in this period.
postedAfterstringoptionalExact cut-off date. Use a date (2026-10-01) or a relative age such as '3 days' or '2 weeks'. Works together with 'Posted within'; the stricter of the two applies.
sortBystringoptionalHow saved posts are ordered for each account. 'Most liked' and 'Newest first' look at every post found before keeping the top ones.
minPlayCountintegeroptionalSkip posts with fewer views than this. Leave empty to keep posts of any size.
searchDepthstringoptional'Standard' runs one search per account and reads every page TikTok returns. 'Deep' adds searches by plain handle, display name and #handle, a most-liked pass, several date ranges and the Photo tab, then merges them without duplicates. Deep finds noticeably more mentions but takes a few minutes per account. Set the run memory to at least 2048 MB for deep search.
searchCountrystringoptionalTikTok shows different posts in different countries. Pick a country to see the mentions people there get in their search results (for example Japan for Japanese posts). 'Any' uses the fastest connection. Runs with a country are slower.
followRelatedVideosbooleanoptionalAfter searching, open TikTok's related videos for every post that tags the account and keep related posts that also mention it (repeating a few hops). Often finds many extra tagged posts, mostly from creators who tag the account regularly. Slower, and needs at least 4096 MB of run memory.
maxResultsPerProfileintegeroptionalStop after this many saved posts for each account. TikTok search usually yields tens to a few hundred posts per account, so very high values simply mean 'everything found'.
maxItemsintegeroptionalOptional limit across all accounts in this run. Leave empty for no overall limit.
downloadVideosbooleanoptionalDownload each saved video into this run's key-value store and add a permanent link to it (downloadedVideoUrl). TikTok's own video links only play inside TikTok, so turn this on when you need playable files. It makes runs slower and uses storage.
addPostCountrybooleanoptionalLook up the country TikTok records for every saved post (locationCreated, e.g. US, BR, JP). Useful for market breakdowns of who mentions you. Adds a few seconds per post.

Published README

Read the full published documentation

TikTok Verified Mention Tracker

See who is talking about a TikTok brand or creator account. Give the tracker one or more handles and it collects public TikTok posts that tag the account or type its @handle in the caption. Every row says why it matched, so a real clickable tag never gets confused with a caption that just happens to contain the name.

Use it for brand monitoring, sponsorship and campaign proof, influencer discovery and competitor watch. No TikTok login, no cookies and no setup are needed.

What this actor does

  • Real mentions, with the reason. mentionType labels every row tagged, captionText or searchResult. Tagged rows carry the exact tag and its position in the caption, so a clickable tag is never confused with a typed @handle or a related post.
  • Proof tied to the account. Each row carries the tracked account's TikTok ID, display name, verified badge and follower count, so a renamed or look-alike handle can't slip in. The account's own posts are skipped (isOwnPost).
  • Controls for real monitoring. Date windows from 24 hours to 6 months or an exact cut-off, relevance/most-liked/newest order, minimum views, about 50 country views, and a deep multi-search mode (handle, name, display name, #handle, most-liked pass, photo posts) that merges results without duplicates. Give deep runs 2048 MB of memory.
  • Full post and profile detail. Caption, hashtags, every tagged account, post date, views/likes/comments/shares/saves/reposts, author profile, sound, video or slideshow media, location and ad, paid-partnership and AI labels. Optional permanent video files and related-video discovery.
  • Empty fields are omitted. Fields TikTok doesn't provide for a post are left out instead of filled with placeholders.

Output per post

One row per post, per tracked account. Fields TikTok doesn't provide for a post are left out of that row rather than filled with placeholders.

Use cases

  • Brand and sponsorship monitoring: watch who tags a brand handle, flag paid-partnership posts and keep a permanent copy of the video as proof.
  • Influencer discovery: find creators who repeatedly tag a product or account, ranked by views or likes.
  • Campaign reporting: export post-level engagement (views, likes, comments, shares, saves, reposts) and captions for dashboards.
  • Competitor watch: see which creators a competitor's account tags and how often, or monitor several accounts in one run.
  • Market research: search from a chosen country and compare which creators and posts mention a handle in each market.

FAQ

Do I need a TikTok account, cookies or a proxy? No. The tracker reads what TikTok shows logged-out visitors and picks its connection automatically.

How many mentions can I expect per account? TikTok's search returns tens to a few hundred posts per account. Popular brands usually get dozens of tagged posts; small accounts may get a handful or none. Deep search typically finds 30-60% more posts than standard. The 500 limit is a cap, not a promise.

Why are some rows captionText instead of tagged? TikTok only creates a clickable tag when the creator picks the account from the suggestion list while writing. Typing @nike by hand leaves plain text, so the post is a mention but not a tag.

Why does the caption show "@Gymshark" when the handle is "gymshark"? Tags display the account's name, not always its handle. mentionEntry.username always holds the real handle and displayText holds what the caption shows.

What does "Also follow related videos" add? TikTok search returns only a share of the posts that tag an account. With this option on, the tracker opens the related videos of every tagged post and keeps the ones that also mention the account, a few hops deep. In our tests it roughly doubled the tagged posts found for accounts that already had some, mostly from creators who tag the account regularly. Those rows have foundVia: "relatedVideos". It makes runs slower and needs at least 4096 MB of memory (set it under the run options); with less memory the step is skipped and the run says so.

How do I check sponsored posts? Filter on isPaidPartnership: it is true exactly when TikTok shows the "Paid partnership" label on the post. Combine it with mentionType: "tagged" to list paid posts that tag your brand.

Do the video links work outside TikTok? TikTok's own media links that point at video or sound files (video.playUrl, video.downloadAddr, music.playUrl and the quality variants in video.bitrateInfo) are signed, expire (see mediaUrlExpiresAt) and usually only play inside TikTok. Turn on "Save video files" to get a permanent, playable copy in downloadedVideoUrl. Cover images, avatars, sound covers and slideshow photos open anywhere.

Can I search by country or language? Yes. Set "Search from country" to see the posts TikTok shows people in that market, for example Japan or Brazil. Runs with a country take longer. To see where each post was actually made, also turn on "Add the country each post was made in" (locationCreated); textLanguage gives the caption language.

Can I monitor accounts on a schedule? Yes. Schedule the task with postedAfter set to your interval (for example 1 day), and deduplicate by postId across runs.

Is this affiliated with TikTok? No. It is an independent tool that reads public TikTok pages.

Known limits

  • TikTok decides how deep a search goes. Each search stops when TikTok has nothing more to show, usually after tens to a couple of hundred posts. Deep search combines several searches to go further.
  • Most search results are related posts, not tags. Only a small share of what TikTok returns for a handle actually tags it. That is why rows are labelled with mentionType, why the default keeps only real mentions, and why "Also follow related videos" exists. Accounts that no search result tags get nothing extra from it.
  • Results depend on time and region. TikTok personalises search, so two runs can return slightly different posts. Pick a "Search from country" to look at one market, and for monitoring schedule runs and deduplicate by postId.
  • Comments are not searched. TikTok offers no way to find comments that mention an account; scanning comments of related posts finds almost none.
  • Counts are a snapshot. Views, likes and comments are as of scrapedAt; schedule runs to build a history.

For AI agents and automations

  • Filter on mentionType instead of reading captions: tagged is TikTok's own tag of the account, captionText is a typed @handle without a working tag, searchResult is only related.
  • Use targetUserId to join rows to an account. Handles can change; IDs don't.
  • Deduplicate across scheduled runs by postId. Engagement numbers are a snapshot at scrapedAt.
  • An empty dataset with a successful run means TikTok had no matching posts. TARGET_SUMMARY tells you whether the account exists and how many posts were checked.

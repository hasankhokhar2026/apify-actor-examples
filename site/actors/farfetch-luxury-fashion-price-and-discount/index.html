    Farfetch Luxury Fashion Price & Discount Extractor is an Apify Actor published by Nimbryxo Studio. It accepts 6 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 6 declared fields, including brand, title, price, discountPercent, gender and productUrl. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Farfetch aggregates luxury fashion from boutiques and designer brands worldwide into one marketplace. This actor searches Farfetch by keyword, brand, gender, new arrivals or sale items — or looks up specific product URLs directly — and returns each item as a structured record with pricing, discount, color and designer details.

    What this actor does

    • Searches Farfetch products by free-text keyword
    • Browses all products from a specific luxury brand
    • Lists new arrivals and currently discounted sale items, optionally filtered by gender
    • Fetches full product details for a list of specific Farfetch product URLs
    • Empty fields are omitted from records — a field only appears when Farfetch actually published that data for the product

    Use cases

    • Price monitoring — track a designer's catalog for price changes and new discounts
    • Trend forecasting — scan new arrivals by gender to see what's launching across brands
    • Sale tracking — pull saleItems regularly to catch deep discounts as they appear
    • Competitive benchmarking — compare the same designer's pricing against other luxury retailers
    • Catalog enrichment — feed a list of product URLs through productDetails to backfill price, color and material data

    Questions

    Do I need a Farfetch account?

    No — only publicly available product listings are read; no login or cookies are required.

    What currency are prices shown in?

    Whatever currency Farfetch returns for the request, reflected in the currency field — check it rather than assuming USD.

    Why does a product have no originalPrice or discountPercent?

    Those fields only appear when the item is actually marked down; full-price items simply don't carry a discount.

    What's the difference between brand and designer?

    brand is the label Farfetch shows on the product card; designer is populated separately when Farfetch's product data distinguishes a specific designer from the brand line.

    Can I combine brand and gender filters?

    Yes — set mode to searchByBrand, fill in brand, and set gender to narrow that brand's catalog to women's, men's, or kids' items.

    How many products can one run return?

    Up to maxItems, capped at 1000.

    Is this affiliated with Farfetch?

    No. This is an independent, third-party actor that reads Farfetch's publicly available product pages; it is not operated by or affiliated with Farfetch.com.

    Farfetch Luxury Fashion Price & Discount Extractor is an Apify Actor published by Nimbryxo Studio. It accepts 14 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 15 declared fields, including brand, title, boutique, styleCode, price and originalPrice. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Farfetch brings together luxury fashion from boutiques and designer brands around the world. This actor searches Farfetch by keyword, browses a brand or a full category, lists new arrivals and sale items, or looks up specific product URLs. Every product comes back as a structured record with local pricing, discount, sizes, color, stock, category, images and product details.

    What this actor does

    • Searches Farfetch products by free-text keyword
    • Browses a designer's catalogue
    • Browses live women's, men's and kids' categories — clothing, shoes, bags, accessories, jewellery, watches, pre-owned, activewear, lingerie and homeware
    • Lists new arrivals and currently discounted sale items, optionally filtered by gender
    • Filters listings by color, price band, discount band and sort order
    • Scrapes any Farfetch listing URL, including sale-category and combined-filter pages
    • Fetches full product details for a list of Farfetch product URLs, including the seller/boutique and brand style code
    • Supports 17 regional storefronts with local currencies, including USD, GBP, EUR, HKD, CNY, SGD, JPY and more
    • Empty fields are omitted — a field only appears when Farfetch published that data for the product

    Data source

    Farfetch's global farfetch.com host blocks automated HTTP access. This actor uses Farfetch's publicly accessible regional storefronts on farfetch.cn, which expose the same global product IDs through server-rendered product pages — no login, account or cookies required. Product pages also embed the seller (boutique) and brand style code in their hydration state, so those fields are read without extra requests.

    Prices, discounts and availability are local to the selected storefront. Use the locale input to choose the market and currency. For USD, use nz; for GBP, uk; for EUR, choose fr, de, it, es or ie.

    Use cases

    • Price monitoring — track a designer's catalog or a whole category for price changes and new discounts in a chosen market
    • Sale tracking — pull sale items or discount bands regularly to catch markdowns as they appear
    • Category research — compare pricing and stock across women's, men's and kids' categories in different markets
    • Trend forecasting — scan new arrivals by gender to see what's launching across brands
    • Catalog enrichment — feed product URLs through productDetails to backfill price, sizes, color, condition, category and material data

    Questions

    Do I need a Farfetch account or cookies?

    No. Only publicly available product listings are read; no login, account or cookies are required.

    Which market and currency should I use?

    Use the locale that matches your target market. nz returns USD, uk returns GBP, fr/de/it/es/ie return EUR, and the other options return their local currencies. Farfetch prices are market-local, so the same product can cost different amounts in different storefronts.

    Why does a product have no originalPrice or discountPercent?

    Those fields only appear when the item is actually marked down; full-price items simply don't carry a discount.

    What do category and categories mean?

    Farfetch groups products in a breadcrumb below the brand. categories keeps the full trail and category is the deepest one, so a bag can come back as ["Bags", "Shoulder Bags"] and Shoulder Bags.

    What is condition?

    Farfetch marks new products as new and pre-owned products as used. Pre-owned listings often also have a Pre-Owned category in the breadcrumb.

    Can I filter by size?

    Size filtering is not exposed as an input. Farfetch's listing URL accepts only opaque internal numeric size IDs; letter sizes return a server error and the same ID means different labels on different size scales. Sizes are reported per product in sizes and per variant in variants, so filter the dataset on those fields after the run.

    Can I combine filters?

    Yes — category, brand or search mode can be combined with gender, sortBy, colors, priceMin, priceMax and discountRange in one run.

    How many products can one run return?

    Up to maxItems, capped at 2500. Listings return up to 180 products per page and paginate until the items run out; the source has no fixed page ceiling. For more than 2500, run separate categories, brands, price bands or discount bands and combine the datasets — deduplicate on productId.

    Do I get the boutique and brand style code?

    Yes, in productDetails mode: boutique is the seller name and styleCode is the brand style ID. Farfetch-owned products do not publish a boutique name, so that field is omitted for those records.

    Why do some category URLs return no products?

    Farfetch uses different category paths in different markets. Use the category dropdown, which contains only verified live paths, or search by keyword or brand.

    Does Farfetch publish product ratings or reviews?

    No. The regional product pages carry no per-product rating, review count or review text; the "4.7/5 stars" copy is Farfetch's site-wide marketing, not a product rating. Those fields are therefore not emitted.

    Are prices the same as farfetch.com US?

    No. The global farfetch.com host blocks automated access, so this actor uses Farfetch's regional storefronts. Prices and availability are local to the selected storefront and should be treated as market-specific.

    Is this affiliated with Farfetch?

    No. This is an independent, third-party actor that reads Farfetch's publicly available product pages; it is not operated by or affiliated with Farfetch.

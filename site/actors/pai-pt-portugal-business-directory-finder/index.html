Each result can carry up to 9 declared fields, including name, categories, city, street, phone and website. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Pai.pt (Páginas Amarelas) lists businesses across every district of Portugal, but browsing by trade and city on the site itself means paging through results by hand. This actor searches pai.pt by category and city or concelho — or nationwide — and can enrich each result with the fuller profile data (email, website, coordinates, tags) that only lives on the business's own page.

    What this actor does

    • Searches by business category and a Portuguese city or concelho, or leaves location blank to search all of Portugal at once.
    • Offers over 30 curated categories and 34 curated cities/concelhos as ready-to-pick options, with free-text overrides for any category or location slug not on those lists.
    • Optionally visits each result's own profile page to add email, website, GPS coordinates, full category/tag lists, and an "about" description.
    • Filters results to businesses currently open (computed from their published hours in the Europe/Lisbon timezone) or at or above a minimum star rating.
    • Only includes a field when pai.pt actually published that value for the business, rather than sending it empty.

    Use cases

    • Trade lead generation — build a prospect list of electricians, lawyers, or restaurants by concelho for outreach.
    • Local market research — compare business density and ratings for a category across Portuguese cities.
    • Directory or comparison-site building — feed structured Portuguese business listings into your own local-search product.
    • Competitor mapping — see who else operates in a category and area, with hours and contact details.
    • Franchise and site-selection analysis — check how saturated a concelho already is for a service before opening there.

    Questions

    Where does this data come from, and is it affiliated with pai.pt?

    It reads the public search and business-profile pages on pai.pt (Páginas Amarelas), Portugal's business directory. This actor is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by pai.pt.

    Why do some businesses have no rating?

    Pai.pt only shows a rating once a business has received at least one review, so a business with none simply has no rating/reviewCount fields rather than a misleading 0.

    What's the difference between category and categories?

    category is the single text label shown on the search card. categories is the full list of categories registered on the business's profile page, and only appears when a profile is fetched (fetchDetails: true, or always in detail mode).

    Can I search all of Portugal instead of one city?

    Yes — leave location blank to search a category nationwide instead of scoping to a city or concelho.

    Why is fetchDetails on by default?

    Email, website, coordinates, and full category/tag lists only live on a business's own profile page. The search card already carries phone and address, so those fields populate even with fetchDetails: false — turn it off only if you want a faster scan and don't need the rest.

    How is isOpenNow worked out?

    This actor computes it from the business's own published opening hours, checked against the current time in the Europe/Lisbon timezone. If no hours are published, the field is left off rather than guessed.

    What happens with a category or location that doesn't exist on pai.pt?

    An unrecognized category returns zero results. An unrecognized location is handled by pai.pt itself, which falls back to a nationwide result set for the category rather than erroring — double-check your location slug if you need city-scoped results.

    Why does category: "dentistas" combined with a location return nothing?

    This is a quirk of pai.pt's own category tree, not an actor bug. Individual dentists on pai.pt are filed under the broader "clinicas-e-especialidades-medicas" category and only tagged with the keyword "dentista" — there's no dentistas node for a specific city. The nationwide dentistas search still works because pai.pt resolves it as a keyword search across the whole country. To find dentists in a specific city, search dentistas nationwide and filter the results by city yourself, or use customCategory: "clinicas-e-especialidades-medicas" with a location (note this broader category covers other medical specialties too, not dentists exclusively).

Search IMDb movies and TV shows, fetch titles by IMDb ID, browse Top 250 and regional charts, or run an advanced search by genre, year, rating and vote count, with cast, crew and plot details per title.

IMDb Search, Charts, Reviews & Episodes Scraper

IMDb spreads search, charts, title details, user reviews and episode lists across separate pages with no bulk export. This actor collects them into one dataset: search results, direct title lookups, nine official charts, the popularity meter, filtered advanced searches, user reviews, and episode lists for TV series — each row carrying the metadata IMDb publishes for it.

What this actor does

  • Searches IMDb's full title catalog by keyword, with optional type, genre, year, rating and vote filters
  • Fetches specific titles directly by IMDb ID or title URL
  • Pulls one of nine IMDb charts, including Top 250, Top 250 TV, Bottom 100, English-language and regional Indian charts
  • Returns IMDb's current popularity-meter titles
  • Runs a filtered, sortable advanced search by title type, genre, country, language, runtime, release year, minimum rating and minimum vote count
  • Exports user reviews with reviewer name, reviewer rating, review title and body, helpfulness votes, spoiler flag and submission date
  • Lists episodes of a TV series with season and episode numbers, episode rating, plot, runtime, certificate, poster and credits
  • Enriches title rows with rating breakdown, keywords, production companies, alternative titles (AKAs), detailed release dates, trailers, awards, box office (including worldwide gross) and popularity rank
  • Omits a field when IMDb did not publish a value for it

One dataset, three row types

Every row has a recordType:

recordType What the row represents
title A movie, TV series, short, video game or other title
review One IMDb user review of a title

Use cases

  • Recommendation engines — export a chart with ratings, genres, keywords and rating breakdowns to seed a model
  • Catalog enrichment — fetch metadata for a known list of IMDb IDs and fill in cast, companies, AKAs, release dates and box office including worldwide gross
  • Review analysis — export reviews with reviewer ratings, helpfulness votes and spoiler flags for sentiment or topic research
  • TV episode guides — list every episode of a series with season/episode numbers, ratings, plots and credits
  • Genre, language and era research — run a filtered advanced search by genre, country, language, runtime, year or vote level to study which titles rate highest
  • Watchlist building — combine type, genre, rating and vote filters to find well-reviewed titles you have missed
  • Regional content discovery — pull a language-specific chart like Tamil or Malayalam alongside the global Top 250
  • Awards and box office research — compare wins, nominations, budgets, domestic/international/worldwide grosses and opening weekends

FAQ

Is this affiliated with IMDb? No. This is an independent data extraction tool and has no connection to IMDb.com or Amazon.

Do I need an IMDb account or API key? No — nothing needs to be configured beyond the mode and filters above.

How fresh is the data? Each run reads IMDb's current data at the time it executes, so ratings, vote counts and chart positions reflect that moment.

How many reviews can I export? maxItems caps the total rows per run (up to 1000). When you pass several IMDb IDs, the budget is shared across them. Reviews are paginated 50 at a time, so titles with many reviews can fill large exports. Sort and spoiler filters apply to every requested title.

Why do some reviews have no authorRating? IMDb allows reviewers to submit text without a numeric score; those reviews simply omit the field.

Why does episodes mode return nothing? Episodes mode expects TV series IDs. A movie ID has no episode list. Try a series such as tt0903747.

Can I get individual TV episode records? Yes — use episodes mode with the series ID. You can also fetch a known episode ID directly with byId.

Why are some titles missing box office, awards or trailers? IMDb does not publish every field for every title. Box office is a theatrical-film field, so TV series and streaming titles normally have none; older, regional or obscure releases may also lack it. The actor leaves those fields out instead of guessing.

Why is the cast list capped? To keep rows a manageable size while covering the top-billed cast, which is the most commonly needed slice. Episode rows use the same cast cap.

Are trailer playback URLs included? No. Trailer rows include a stable IMDb video page URL, title, type, thumbnail and runtime, not expiring video-file links.

What is popularityRank? It is the title's current position on IMDb's popularity meter; lower numbers mean more popular.

What is the difference between topRated and advanced? topRated returns one of IMDb's fixed chart lists in chart order; advanced runs a filtered, sortable search across IMDb's full catalog using the criteria you specify.

How do I find a title's IMDb ID? It is in the title's IMDb page URL, e.g. imdb.com/title/tt0111161/ — pass it with or without the tt prefix, or paste the full URL.

Can I mix title, review and episode rows in one run? Each run uses one mode and produces one row type, except that byId can return any title-shaped record. Use the recordType field when combining exports from several runs.

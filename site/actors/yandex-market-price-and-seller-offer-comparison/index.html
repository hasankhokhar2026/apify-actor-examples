    Yandex Market Price & Seller Offer Comparison Scraper is an Apify Actor published by Nimbryxo Studio. It accepts 12 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 45 declared fields, including recordType, productId, marketSku, name, price and originalPrice. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Pull product listings, pricing, and reviews from Yandex Market, the marketplace where most listings are fulfilled by multiple independent sellers competing on the same product page. Search by keyword, browse a category, compare every seller's price for one product, or collect reviews.

    What this actor does

    • Searches Yandex Market by one or more keywords and returns matching product cards
    • Browses products listed under a category URL
    • Fetches a single product's full detail page together with competing seller offers
    • Collects reviews for a product or for a seller's storefront
    • Uses cookieless HTTP first and parses Yandex Market's SSR data-zone-data payloads
    • Retries with fresh sessions on robot/CAPTCHA pages and only then uses its automatic residential-proxy and browser fallbacks
    • Emits diagnostic records for blocked pages and partial products instead of silently returning false empty results
    • Omits empty fields from records

    Use cases

    • Price monitoring — track how a product's price moves across every seller offering it
    • Seller benchmarking — compare seller ratings, delivery times, and pricing side by side
    • Catalog research — pull a whole category's listings with current prices and availability
    • Sentiment tracking — gather product reviews with pros/cons to spot recurring complaints
    • Market-entry analysis — scope typical price ranges and seller competition before listing a product

    Questions

    Does searchQueries accept Russian text?

    Yes — keywords can be in Russian or English; Yandex Market returns whatever matches on its own site.

    How do I see every seller's price for one product, not just the lead offer?

    Use mode: "getProductDetails" with the product URL. The product record contains the nested offers array, and each offer is also emitted as a flat recordType: "offer" record. maxItems caps how many offers are collected.

    Why does a searchProducts record have no offers field?

    Search and category listing cards only show the lead offer; the full offer comparison is fetched only in getProductDetails mode. Listing cards may also omit rating, seller, or stock when Yandex does not expose them in the SSR payload.

    Can I get reviews for a seller instead of a product?

    Yes — pass a storefront URL in sellerUrls with mode: "getReviews"; those records carry reviewType: "seller" and seller identifying fields instead of product ones.

    Do I need to configure the proxy myself?

    No. Listing modes are HTTP-first and cookieless. If a request is proven blocked, the actor automatically tries a residential proxy and then a browser fallback. You can still provide proxyConfiguration to override the automatic fallback.

    What happens if a page is blocked or a run finds nothing?

    Blocked pages produce diagnostic records with the failing stage and reason. Partial products are marked partial: true and keep the fields that were readable. If the whole run produces no product, offer, or review records and no diagnostics, the dataset gets a single informational record (type: "yandex_market_scraper_error").

    What are the hard limits?

    Yandex Market SSR usually exposes roughly 8–20 listing cards per page; pagination stops when a page adds no new records. Product, offers, and reviews pages are more CAPTCHA-prone than listing pages, so the actor retries fresh sessions and then escalates to its proxy/browser fallbacks. The SSR offers page repeats its first 16 offers when paginated, so this version can collect at most the offers that page exposes; maxItems caps collection below that. Reviews beyond the first SSR reviews page are not available in this version. Prices are in RUB and use the current card price when Yandex shows one, with the regular price as originalPrice.

    Is this affiliated with Yandex?

    No. This actor reads Yandex Market's publicly accessible pages and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Yandex.

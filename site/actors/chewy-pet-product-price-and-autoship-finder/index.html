    Chewy Pet Product Price & Autoship Finder is an Apify Actor published by Nimbryxo Studio. It accepts 13 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 8 declared fields, including name, brand, price, autoshipPrice, rating and reviewCount. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Chewy is one of the largest US online pet retailers, selling food, treats, toys and supplies for dogs, cats and other pets. This actor searches Chewy by keyword, category URL, brand name or a specific list of product URLs, and returns each product as a structured record with pricing, Autoship subscription price, rating and variant detail.

    What this actor does

    • Searches Chewy products by keyword, with optional pet-type and sort filters
    • Browses every product in a given category URL
    • Pulls every product listed under a specific brand name
    • Enriches a list of specific Chewy product URLs with full detail, including size/flavor variants
    • Empty fields are omitted from records — a field only appears when Chewy actually shows that data for the product

    Use cases

    • Price monitoring — track a watch-list of products for price or Autoship-discount changes
    • Brand catalog audits — pull every product from a brand to check pricing and stock consistency
    • Category benchmarking — compare price-per-unit across a whole category like dry dog food
    • Variant research — enrich product URLs to see every size/flavor option and its individual price
    • Review tracking — snapshot rating and reviewCount over time to watch sentiment trends

    Questions

    Do I need a Chewy account?

    No — only publicly available product listings are read; no login or API key is required.

    What is Autoship, and what does autoshipPrice mean?

    Autoship is Chewy's recurring-delivery subscription, usually priced lower than a one-time purchase. autoshipPrice is that subscription price when the product offers it; isAutoship tells you whether the option exists at all.

    Why does a product have no productVariants?

    That field is only populated when scraping product detail pages (byProductUrls mode or detail enrichment); search and category listing cards don't expose per-variant pricing.

    Can I filter by multiple pet types at once?

    petType accepts one value per run; to cover several pet types, run the actor once per type.

    What's the difference between search and byCategory mode?

    search uses Chewy's keyword search and respects petType/sortBy; byCategory walks a specific category URL directly, which is useful for exhaustively covering one product category.

    How many products can one run return?

    Up to maxItems, capped at 5000.

    Is this affiliated with Chewy?

    No. This is an independent, third-party actor that reads Chewy.com's publicly available product pages; it is not operated by or affiliated with Chewy, Inc.

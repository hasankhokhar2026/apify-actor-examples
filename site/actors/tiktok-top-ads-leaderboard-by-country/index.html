    TikTok Top Ads Leaderboard - 122 Markets & Metrics is an Apify Actor published by Nimbryxo Studio. It accepts 14 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 12 declared fields, including adId, adTitle, brandName, ctr, like and industryName. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    See what is actually winning on TikTok. This actor reads TikTok's public Creative Center Top Ads boards and returns one structured row per ad: the creative, brand, CTR, likes, budget tier, industry, campaign objective, downloadable video renditions, and the landing page when TikTok publishes it. It also reads TikTok's handpicked Top Ads Spotlight and can refresh a list of known ad IDs.

    No TikTok account, cookies, API key, or browser is required. The actor signs its own anonymous Creative Center session with plain HTTP.

    What this actor does

    • Reads the Top Ads leaderboard for any of 122 markets, 7 / 30 / 180-day windows, 258 industries, 8 campaign objectives, 17 ad languages, Spark/Non-Spark formats, and 5 like-percentile tiers.
    • Ranks by TikTok's For You mix, Reach, CTR, 2-second view rate, 6-second view rate, conversion rate, or Likes. CTR and Likes are returned highest-first.
    • Goes beyond TikTok's 20-ad board by reading more filter combinations and deduplicating by ad ID. It stops at your maxItems or when the source has no more unique ads.
    • Optionally adds the ad detail record: landing page, target countries, comments, shares, ad source, and the full objective list.
    • Optionally adds the per-second retention curve from TikTok's keyframe data.
    • Reads the Top Ads Spotlight: five handpicked ads with TikTok's expert commentary.
    • Refreshes specific ad IDs or Creative Center URLs without re-running a whole board.
    • Preserves the original output fields and adds new fields beside them.

    Modes

    Mode What it reads
    Top Ads leaderboard (default) The main Top Ads boards, with all filters and the sweep.
    Top Ads Spotlight TikTok's handpicked Spotlight list, five ads with expert commentary. industry is the only board filter used; includeDetails adds title, brand, landing page, and target countries.
    Specific ad IDs A detail lookup for each ID or Creative Center URL you provide.

    Data source

    TikTok Creative Center Top Ads at https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/topads/pc/en and Top Ads Spotlight at https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/tiktok-topads-spotlight/pc/en. The actor calls the same public JSON endpoints the pages use and reads only publicly available data.

    Use cases

    • Creative research - build a swipe file of winning hooks, formats, and CTAs by market, industry, objective, and language.
    • Competitor monitoring - see which brands and landing pages are appearing in the Top Ads boards for your vertical.
    • Benchmarking - compare your CTR and likes with the top percentile bands TikTok publishes.
    • Trend tracking - run weekly with period=7 and watch new ads enter the board.
    • Creative briefs - pull the retention curve to show where viewers drop off.

    Output

    Every row keeps the original fields: adId, adUrl, adTitle, brandName, cost, ctr, like, industryKey, objectiveKey, isSearch, observedCountry, observedPeriod, scrapedAt, videoCoverUrl, videoDuration, videoHeight, videoWidth, videoId, and videoUrls.

    New fields are additive:

    • source, creativeCenterUrl, rank, foundInCountry
    • costTier, industryName, parentIndustryName, objectiveName, adFormat
    • landingPageUrl, targetCountries, commentCount, shareCount, adSource, objectives, voiceOver
    • spotlightHighlight, retentionCurve
    • videoUrlBest, videoUrlsExpireAt
    • detailsFetched, detailUnavailable, availableTotal

    Empty fields are omitted. 0 and false are kept because they are real values. brandName="Not Mention" and similar placeholders are omitted.

    Notes on the metrics

    • ctr is TikTok's CTR score in percent units: 0.62 means 0.62%.
    • cost is a budget tier, not money: 0 = Low, 1 = Medium, 2 = High.
    • like is an absolute count.
    • commentCount and shareCount are detail fields; 0 is common and real.
    • videoUrls are signed CDN URLs with a limited lifetime. videoUrlsExpireAt tells you when the earliest one expires. Download what you want to keep.
    • availableTotal is TikTok's ranked-index size for the base query, not the number of rows the actor delivered.

    Questions

    Do I need a TikTok account or cookies?

    No. The actor signs an anonymous Creative Center session itself. It never asks for a TikTok login, cookie, or API key.

    Why did I get fewer rows than maxItems?

    TikTok exposes 20 ads per board and up to 500 per query. The actor reads extra boards when sweep is on, but some filter combinations have fewer unique ads. The run status message reports how many boards were read and why it stopped.

    Can I search by keyword?

    TikTok's keyword search on Top Ads is login-only; an anonymous request returns zero ads. This actor does not ship a keyword field. Use industry, objective, language, or a specific ad ID instead.

    Why is brandName missing on some rows?

    TikTok often does not publish a brand name. It is present on roughly a quarter of rows in our sample. The actor omits the field rather than guessing.

    Why does adUrl open the Top Ads dashboard?

    TikTok has no stable public per-ad deep link; its own ad detail links redirect to the dashboard. adId is the stable key. creativeCenterUrl uses TikTok's canonical ad route for reference.

    Are the video URLs permanent?

    No. They are signed CDN URLs that expire within hours. Download them promptly or use videoUrlsExpireAt.

    Why do some industries return no ads?

    The 258-entry taxonomy includes niche child categories that TikTok may not have a board for in a given market and period. Try the parent industry or a longer period.

    Is this affiliated with TikTok?

    No. This is an independent actor that reads publicly available Creative Center data. It is not endorsed by or affiliated with TikTok or ByteDance.

    Limits and notes

    How many ads can I get?

    TikTok shows an anonymous visitor 20 ads per filter board (one market + period + industry + objective + ranking + format + like tier). The second page is empty. The ranked index behind a query can hold up to 500 ads, but only 20 are visible per board.

    This actor reads board after board - other rankings, objectives, industries, formats, like tiers, languages, and markets - and deduplicates by ad ID. maxItems is a cap on unique ads, not a promise that one board contains that many. If the source runs out, the run succeeds with an honest status message instead of pretending it filled the request.

    Limitations

    • 20 ads per board, 500 per query.
    • Keyword search is login-only and is not supported.
    • Brand name and landing page are published for only some ads.
    • Exact spend, impressions, reach, dates, and audience targeting are not in the public Creative Center payload.
    • Media URLs expire.
    • TikTok may change its internal signing scheme; the actor re-mints and can recover the signer from the live page bundle, but a future change can still require an update.

    Hiring.cafe Job Listings & Salary Filter Scraper is an Apify Actor published by Nimbryxo Studio. It accepts 26 input fields, none of them required at the top level. Each result can carry up to 32 declared fields, including title, companyName, workplaceType, seniorityLevel, commitment and salaryMin. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Hiring.cafe aggregates job postings pulled directly from Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, Workday, SuccessFactors, BambooHR, and thousands of other applicant-tracking systems and company career pages. This actor queries that aggregated index and returns structured job records, so you can filter on dimensions — salary band, security clearance, required tech stack — that most job boards don't expose in their own search UI.

    What this actor does

    • Runs one or more keyword searches against hiring.cafe's job index and collects matching postings.
    • Narrows results by workplace type, seniority, commitment type, years of experience, security clearance, and salary range/currency/frequency.
    • Matches separately against job title text, job description text, parsed technology tags, and parsed requirement bullets, so you can target precise phrasing rather than a single blended search box.
    • Includes company-level filters (whitelist/blacklist by name or keyword, industry include/exclude) so you can scope a run to or away from specific employers.
    • Returns one record per job with company, compensation, and location detail; fields the source doesn't provide for a given job are left out of that record.

    Use cases

    • Talent intelligence – track hiring velocity for a role, company, or region over time.
    • Compensation benchmarking – aggregate disclosed salary ranges by role, seniority, and location.
    • Remote-work research – isolate workplaceTypes: ["Remote"] postings to study the remote-first job market.
    • ATS/tooling market share – group results by source to see which applicant-tracking systems dominate hiring.
    • Skills-demand tracking – aggregate technicalTools across postings to spot which technologies are rising in demand.
    • Targeted job alerts – run narrow filters (keyword + location + seniority) on a schedule to catch new matching postings.

    Questions

    Does this cover jobs outside the US?

    Yes. hiring.cafe aggregates listings globally; use locations, workplaceCountries in the output, or leave locations empty for worldwide results.

    Why do some jobs have salaryMin/salaryMax of 0?

    Many postings don't disclose compensation. When a listing has no published salary, both fields come back as 0. Set onlyTransparentSalaries to true to keep only listings that publish a range.

    What's the difference between seniorityLevel and minYearsExperience?

    seniorityLevel is hiring.cafe's categorical tag (e.g. Mid Level, Senior Level). minYearsExperience is the numeric minimum years parsed from the job description text, and may be 0 when the posting doesn't state a number.

    How do searchQueries and the other text filters interact?

    searchQueries is the general keyword search hiring.cafe runs against the whole posting. jobTitleQuery, jobDescriptionQuery, technologyKeywordsQuery, and requirementsKeywordsQuery are additional, more targeted filters applied on top of that search.

    Can I pull postings from one employer only?

    Yes, set companyNames to that employer's name (or use companyKeywords for a partial match). Use excludedCompanyNames/excludedCompanyKeywords to do the opposite.

    Does dateFetchedPastNDays reflect the original posting date?

    No — it reflects when hiring.cafe's own index last fetched the listing, which is the freshness signal the source provides.

    Are expired listings included?

    hiring.cafe's active search only surfaces current postings, so isExpired is normally false; the field is kept in the output for completeness.

    Is this affiliated with hiring.cafe?

    No. This actor is an independent tool that queries hiring.cafe's public job search and is not affiliated with or endorsed by hiring.cafe.

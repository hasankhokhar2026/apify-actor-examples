    PriceCharting Game Value Guide: Loose, CIB & Graded is an Apify Actor published by Nimbryxo Studio. It accepts 5 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 6 declared fields, including title, consoleName, loosePrice, cibPrice, newPrice and gameUrl. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    PriceCharting tracks market prices for video games across every major console, broken out by condition — loose, complete-in-box, new, and graded. This actor pulls that price ladder into structured records by game title search, by browsing a specific console's full price list, or by looking up one game's page directly.

    What this actor does

    • Searches PriceCharting by game title, optionally narrowed to one console.
    • Browses every priced game listed for a single console, page by page.
    • Looks up one game's full price breakdown directly from its PriceCharting page URL.
    • Returns whichever condition prices PriceCharting has for that game — loose, complete-in-box, new, graded, box-only, and manual-only — all in US dollars.
    • Leaves a price field out of the record when PriceCharting doesn't track that condition for the game, instead of showing a zero.

    Use cases

    • Reselling and flipping — check current loose and CIB prices before buying a collection or lot.
    • Collection valuation — price out a personal collection for insurance or resale.
    • Price-trend tracking — rerun the same game or console over time to watch prices move.
    • Grading ROI research — compare loosePrice/cibPrice against gradedPrice to judge whether grading a copy is worth it.
    • Platform-wide market research — pull an entire console's price list to compare values across a generation.

    Questions

    Do I need an account or API key?

    No. PriceCharting's price pages are publicly accessible without authentication.

    What currency are the prices in?

    US dollars — PriceCharting's price guide is denominated in USD.

    What does CIB mean?

    "Complete In Box" — the game includes its original box and manual along with the cartridge or disc, as opposed to a loose copy with just the game itself.

    What is a graded price?

    It's the price for a copy that's been professionally graded for condition (for example by WATA or VGA), which can carry a significant premium over an ungraded copy.

    Why don't all games have every price field?

    Not every title has recorded sales for every condition — a game with no tracked graded sales, for instance, simply won't have a gradedPrice in its record.

    What's the difference between searchGames and consolePrices?

    searchGames matches a title (optionally narrowed to one console) and returns the matching games. consolePrices instead walks every game PriceCharting has priced for one console, regardless of title.

    How current are the prices?

    Each run reads PriceCharting's current price pages at the time it runs, so values reflect whatever PriceCharting is showing then.

    Is this affiliated with PriceCharting?

    No. This actor reads PriceCharting's public pages and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to PriceCharting in any way.

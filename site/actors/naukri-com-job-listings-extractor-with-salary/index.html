    Naukri.com Job Listings Extractor with Salary & Skills is an Apify Actor published by Nimbryxo Studio. It accepts 15 input fields, none of them required at the top level. Each result can carry up to 53 declared fields, including title, companyName, location, experience, salary and workMode. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Extract Naukri.com job listings as structured data. Search by keyword and Indian city, pull jobs by ID, or monitor a company — then get the full job description, raw and parsed salary, experience bounds, skills, work mode, education requirements, role taxonomy, company ratings and walk-in details. No Naukri login, cookies or API key required.

    What this actor does

    • Three ways to find jobs: keyword and city searches, direct job IDs, or company IDs.
    • Up to 10,000 records per run, with a 3,000-job ceiling per individual query (Naukri returns 20 jobs per page and allows 150 pages).
    • Full detail per job: not just the search card — the actor fetches each posting to return the complete description, salary fields, education, role taxonomy, company profile and walk-in information.
    • Filters: experience in years, salary band, remote or hybrid mode, job age, date or salary sort, internships and walk-in drives.
    • Incremental runs: onlyNew stores emitted job IDs in actor state, so scheduled runs can return only postings you have not seen before.
    • Honest salary data: every record carries the raw salary string and a salaryDisclosed flag. Numeric salaryMin and salaryMax appear only when Naukri discloses a figure.
    • Empty fields are omitted — records never contain null, blank strings or empty arrays.

    Use cases

    • Recruitment agencies — build talent pipelines and track which employers are hiring for a role or city.
    • Job seekers — schedule a daily monitor that returns only newly posted jobs matching a skill set.
    • Compensation teams — collect salary bands and disclosure rates for a role across companies and cities.
    • Market researchers — measure hiring volume by location, industry, role or work mode over time.
    • Sales teams — detect companies ramping up hiring as a buying signal.
    • Data teams — build an India job-market dataset with full descriptions, skills and company profiles.

    Questions

    Do I need a Naukri account, cookies or an API key?

    No. The actor reads publicly available Naukri job data without a login.

    Why is salaryMin missing for most jobs?

    Naukri hides salary on most postings. In a 24-job sample, only 16.7% disclosed a numeric range. Every record still includes the raw salary string and salaryDisclosed so you can separate disclosed jobs from hidden ones.

    Why do some records lack description?

    description is on by default. If Naukri does not return a detail page for a posting, the record falls back to the shorter description from the search result rather than dropping the job.

    How many jobs can one run return?

    maxItems supports up to 10,000. Each individual query is capped by Naukri at 3,000 jobs, so a single keyword-and-city combination tops out there. Add more keywords, cities or companies to reach higher totals.

    Does the work-mode filter include office jobs?

    Naukri only exposes remote and hybrid filters. Use any to return every mode; the output workMode tells you which one each job has.

    Can I filter full-time, part-time or contractual jobs?

    No. Naukri does not honour those filters reliably. The supported job-type filters are internships and walk-in drives. The output jobType and employmentType still describe each posting.

    Are benefits, localities or recruiter details included?

    No. Naukri does not expose benefits, locality lists or recruiter names on these postings, so they are not fabricated or promised.

    Why are companyRating and companyAddress missing on some jobs?

    These come from the employer's AmbitionBox profile and company page. Smaller or newer employers often do not have them.

    Why is applyRedirectUrl missing on most records?

    Naukri only exposes an external apply link for some postings, and those links can be ad-wrapped. applyUrl always gives you a usable link: the company gateway or external redirect when present, otherwise the Naukri listing.

    Can I get walk-in venue and contact details?

    Yes, when Naukri publishes them. They are returned in walkInDetails; not every walk-in posting includes a venue or phone number.

    How fresh is the data?

    Every run queries Naukri live, so results reflect the listings available at run time.

    Do I need to set up a proxy?

    No. The actor chooses a working connection to Naukri automatically.

    Is this affiliated with Naukri.com?

    No. This is an independent third-party tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Naukri.com.

    Limits and notes

    Limitations

    • Salary is disclosed on a minority of postings. Numeric salary fields are therefore conditional, not guaranteed.
    • Benefits, locality lists and recruiter names are not available from Naukri's public job data.
    • External apply links are partial and can be ad-wrapped; applyUrl always provides a fallback.
    • Walk-in venue and contact details are present only when the employer publishes them.
    • The actor covers Naukri's India job market. Gulf listings on other domains are out of scope.
    • A single query returns at most 3,000 jobs (150 pages of 20). Split broad searches by city, keyword or company to collect more.

    Notes on coverage

    Naukri exposes different amounts of detail per posting. The actor returns what the public job record contains and omits fields that are not published; it does not infer salary, benefits, or recruiter details.

    Jumia Africa Catalog & Flash Deals Extractor is an Apify Actor published by Nimbryxo Studio. It accepts 11 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 8 declared fields, including name, price, currency, discountRate, rating and brand. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Jumia runs separate storefronts for ten African markets, each with its own catalog, pricing, and daily deals. This actor pulls structured product data from any of those ten markets — by keyword search, category browse, today's flash sales, or a list of specific product URLs.

    What this actor does

    • Searches a chosen market's catalog by keyword, or browses a whole category by its URL slug.
    • Pulls today's flash/daily-deal products for a market as Jumia currently has them listed.
    • Fetches full product-page details — description and category breadcrumb included — for specific product URLs.
    • Filters results by minimum/maximum price and minimum rating, applied after the data is collected.
    • Returns price, discount, rating, brand, and seller for every product; a field is left out of the record when that market's listing never published it.

    Use cases

    • Cross-market price comparison — compare the same product category's pricing across several African markets.
    • Assortment and catalog research — see what brands and products a category carries in a given country.
    • Deal tracking — pull today's flash sales to catch time-limited discounts before they expire.
    • Seller and brand discovery — collect seller and brand names across a category for market mapping.
    • Price-history building — rerun productDetails on the same URLs over time to track price and discount changes.

    Questions

    Which countries can I scrape?

    Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, Egypt, South Africa, Morocco, Senegal, Cameroon, Tanzania, and Uganda — Jumia's ten active African storefronts.

    How do I find a category slug?

    Open a category on Jumia and copy the part of the URL right after the domain. For https://www.jumia.com.ng/phones-tablets/, the slug is phones-tablets.

    Do I need to log in to use this?

    No. Jumia's product, category, and flash-sale pages are all publicly browsable.

    What's actually different about flashSales mode?

    It reads whichever products Jumia is currently running as flash/daily deals for that market — a different page from the regular catalog, not a filter on search results. The products returned change as Jumia's own deals change.

    Why do description and categories only show up sometimes?

    Those two fields come from the full product page, so they're only populated in productDetails mode. Search, category, and flash-sale listings don't carry that level of detail.

    What currency are prices shown in?

    Each market's own local currency — the currency field tells you which one, matching the country you selected (e.g. NGN for Nigeria, KES for Kenya, ZAR for South Africa).

    Can I combine a price range with a rating filter?

    Yes — minPrice, maxPrice, and minRating can all be set together in any mode that returns multiple products.

    Is this affiliated with Jumia?

    No. This actor reads Jumia's public pages and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Jumia in any way.

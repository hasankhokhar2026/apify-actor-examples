Google Maps Photo Gallery Extractor by Category

Collect public photos and videos from Google Maps places by URL, Place ID, feature ID, CID or search query. Each row carries the image and video links, dimensions, contributor, upload date, owner/visitor flag and place context.

Google Maps Photo Gallery Extractor by Category is an Apify Actor published by Nimbryxo Studio. It accepts 11 input fields, and mode is required. Its build declares a results dataset with 1 view. Runs happen on Apify, where the Store page lists the current price.

Google Maps Photo Gallery Extractor by Category

Collect the public photo and video gallery of Google Maps places without a login, cookie or API key. Paste place links, Place IDs, feature IDs, CIDs or business names, or search for places by keyword, then receive one dataset row per photo, video or nearby Street View panorama with its image link, dimensions, contributor, upload date, owner/visitor flag and place context.

Google's public gallery exposes a finite sample of each place, not the full signed-in gallery. The actor collects media from that sample until it reaches your maxPhotos cap or the sample ends. Google sometimes advertises a larger total than the public response actually serves, so treat maxPhotos, advertisedCount and photosComplete as guidance, not a promise of the complete gallery. Every URL in the dataset is a direct Google-hosted image or video link that can be fetched without a Referer or cookie.

What this actor does

  • Reads photos and videos for one or many places using URLs, share links, ChIJ... Place IDs, 0x...:0x... feature IDs, numeric CIDs or business names
  • Searches Google Maps by keyword and reads the gallery of each matching place
  • Filters by photos, videos, business-owner photos, visitor photos or nearby Street View panoramas
  • Adds contributor name, profile link and ID, exact upload date, image dimensions, video download link and video length when Google publishes them
  • Marks owner-uploaded media when Google publishes enough contributor metadata to make that call
  • Exposes the place identity, categories, address and coordinates on every row so the data joins cleanly to other Google Maps datasets
  • Reports whether each place's result set is complete (photosComplete) and why collection stopped (stopReason)
  • Omits a field when Google does not publish it for that item instead of returning null or a guess

Use cases

  • Menu and storefront monitoring — collect photos for a restaurant, café or retail chain and compare owner media with customer uploads
  • Hospitality and real-estate galleries — build visual datasets for hotels, venues and apartment listings from public place photos
  • Brand consistency audits — track the owner/visitor photo mix and freshness across every branch of a business
  • Computer-vision datasets — full-resolution image URLs with dimensions and place/category context for training and evaluation
  • Local SEO research — compare how competing places present themselves visually, and how recently their galleries changed
  • Travel and directory enrichment — add authentic location imagery to city guides, maps and booking products

FAQ

Do I need a Google account, cookies or an API key? No. The actor reads Google Maps' public place and gallery pages. No login, cookie, API key or proxy configuration is needed.

Why do I get fewer items than maxPhotos? Google's public gallery exposes a finite sample of each place, and some places have very few public photos. The actor collects media from the sample until it reaches your cap or the sample ends. maxPhotos is an upper bound, not a promise. Advertised totals are not reliable: the response sometimes advertises a number that is larger than what it actually serves, so advertisedCount is informational only. Check photosComplete and stopReason on each row to see whether the sample was read completely.

Why are the category tabs from the Google Maps website not all available? The Menu, Food & drink, Vibe, Rooms, Amenities, Exterior, Inside and By owner category identifiers are not exposed by the public photo response this actor reads. Anonymous live probes returned only the All category for landmark, restaurant and hotel test places, while the same places exposed more categories only in a cookie-reference session. The current gallery request ignores those category identifiers, the public per-photo metadata response has no photo-category label, and the retired listentityphotos route is not reachable anonymously. These tabs are a confirmed limit of the cookieless path, not a missing feature. The actor offers photos, videos, by_owner, by_visitors and street_view filters instead and does not claim the other tabs.

How are owner photos identified? Owner classification depends on Google publishing both the business owner's profile and the contributor profile for an item. When both are present, isOwnerPhoto is true or false; when either is missing, the status is unknown, the flag is omitted and the item is not returned by the owner or visitor filter. The classification is only as reliable as that public contributor metadata.

Why can the owner or video filter return fewer rows than requested? These filters search the whole public sample, up to a safety bound, instead of stopping after a small fixed window. Even so, the public sample may contain few owner-uploaded or video items, and a place can simply run out of matches. In that case photosComplete is true when the sample and the required contributor metadata were fully read, while collectedCount stays below your cap. If metadata is missing, the rows are reported as incomplete instead.

Are videos included? Yes. Video rows have recordType: "video", isVideo: true, a direct videoUrl MP4 link when Google provides one, and a video length when it can be read from the file.

What is the street_view filter? It returns Street View panoramas near the place's coordinates. These are nearby 360° imagery rather than photos from the place's own gallery; recordType and isStreetView mark them, and they do not carry the place's placeId or featureId. The filter returns at most maxPhotos panoramas and reports street_view_cap when that cap is reached.

Why is caption or tags missing on many rows? Most Google Maps photos have no caption or tags. The field appears only when the contributor or Google published it.

Are the image and video links permanent? They are public Google-hosted URLs that work without a Referer or cookie. Google can change or remove a photo at any time, so re-run the actor when you need a fresh copy.

Is this affiliated with Google? No. This actor reads publicly visible Google Maps data and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Google.

    LinkedIn Company Pages with Funding & Affiliates is an Apify Actor published by Nimbryxo Studio. It accepts 7 input fields, and companyUrls and cookie are required. Each result can carry up to 7 declared fields, including name, industry, employeeCount, followersCount, websiteUrl and companyUrl. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Give this actor a list of LinkedIn company pages and get back the full "About" page for each one — industry, headcount, headquarters, specialties, logo, and (when LinkedIn surfaces it) Crunchbase-sourced funding history — plus the option to also pull every affiliated company and showcase page linked from that company's profile.

    What this actor does

    • Resolves full LinkedIn company URLs, /about pages, or bare slugs into one structured record per company
    • Adds Crunchbase-style funding data (total raised, round count, last round) when includeFundingDetails is on
    • Can walk out from each input company to also emit its affiliated companies and/or showcase pages as separate records
    • Returns both the exact employee count and LinkedIn's bucketed employee-count range when either is available
    • Only includes a field when LinkedIn actually published that data — no null or placeholder values ever appear

    Use cases

    • Sales intelligence — enrich a target-account list with headcount, HQ, industry, and funding stage before outreach
    • Investor and M&A research — pull funding totals and last-round details across a portfolio or watchlist of companies
    • Corporate structure mapping — use includeAffiliates and includeShowcases to surface a parent company's full family of LinkedIn pages
    • Competitive benchmarking — compare headcount, follower growth, and specialties across a set of competitors
    • Recruiting and market research — gather company-level context (industry, size, locations) for a list of employers

    Questions

    Why is a LinkedIn cookie required?

    Full company-page data, including funding and affiliate links, is only exposed to a signed-in LinkedIn session, so a valid session cookie is mandatory for every run.

    Does this work for private as well as public companies?

    Yes — any company with a LinkedIn page returns the same data a logged-in user would see when visiting that page.

    Why do some companies have no funding fields?

    Funding fields only appear when includeFundingDetails is on and LinkedIn has linked Crunchbase funding data to that company; many companies, especially non-startups, have none to show.

    What's the difference between employeeCount and employeeCountRange?

    employeeCount is LinkedIn's exact reported staff figure; employeeCountRange is the bucketed range (e.g. 10001-50000) LinkedIn uses in search filters. Some companies only expose the range.

    What do affiliatedCompanies and showcasePages return when the toggles are off?

    With includeAffiliates/includeShowcases off, the company's own record still lists its affiliated companies and showcase pages as structured data — turning the toggles on additionally fetches a full separate record for each of those pages.

    Why might a field like phone or stockTicker be missing?

    Those fields only exist for companies that have published that information on their LinkedIn page; most companies don't list a phone number or a ticker, so the field is simply omitted.

    How long does my cookie stay valid?

    A LinkedIn li_at session cookie typically lasts several weeks to months; once it expires, log back into LinkedIn and copy the new value.

    Is this affiliated with LinkedIn?

    No. This is an independent actor that reads data through an authenticated LinkedIn session you provide; it is not endorsed by or affiliated with LinkedIn Corporation.
